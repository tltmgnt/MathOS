# Contributing to MathOS

Thanks for helping improve MathOS. Contributions should make claims easier to inspect, reproduce, test, and responsibly apply.

## Contribution expectations

- Explain the problem a change solves.
- State assumptions and limitations.
- Include a reproducible example or test when practical.
- Distinguish conceptual proposals from implemented and evaluated features.
- Do not include API keys, credentials, private records, or sensitive data.
- Avoid claims of causal impact unless the evidence and study design support them.

## Suggested workflow

1. Open an issue describing the claim, gap, or proposed feature.
2. Describe expected behavior and failure conditions.
3. Submit a focused pull request with documentation and tests where applicable.
4. Record what was verified and what remains untested.

## Review questions

- Can another contributor understand the central mechanism?
- Are the assumptions explicit?
- Are counterexamples or edge cases addressed?
- Is the result transferable, and are its boundaries documented?
- Does the change preserve appropriate human oversight?
