## MathOS v0.1.0 — Research Experiments 001–050

MathOS is exploratory research software for testing arithmetic behavior, reproducible claims and bounded proof/evidence workflows. This release candidate collects **50 numbered experiment protocols with recorded executions**, in two phases:

- **Experiments 001–022 (earlier phase):** arithmetic behavior and activation studies, model/operand-order sensitivity, error analyses, in-sample constant baselines, generalization and prompt-format evaluations. See [original v0.1 research report](../research/MATHOS_RESEARCH_REPORT_v0.1.md).
- **Experiments 023–050 (November 18, 2026 follow-up):** 28 further arithmetic conditions on addition, subtraction, multiplication, zero/negative values, commutativity, associativity, distributivity and prompt transfer. Both DistilGPT2 and sshleifer/tiny-gpt2 were evaluated on 12 cases per condition using a single pre-specified local generator and strict leading-integer parsing. See [protocol](../research/EXPERIMENTS_023_050.md) and [observed results](../research/EXPERIMENTS_023_050_RESULTS.md).

### Observed follow-up findings

Across 336 primary prompts per model, DistilGPT2 produced **7 correct** answers and 32 parseable leading integers; tiny-GPT2 produced **0 correct** and 0 parseable leading integers. All seven DistilGPT2 successes were multiplication-by-zero cases, where a fixed always-zero baseline achieved 12/12 correct. DistilGPT2 yielded 12/12 parseable leading integers for the few-shot addition prompts but 0 correct answers. None of the paired cases had **two** parseable model outputs, so paired consistency is **not estimable**.

These are narrow behavioral observations, not results about all models or claims of internal mathematical reasoning.

The [2026-11-18 GitHub Actions execution](https://github.com/tltmgnt/MathOS/actions/runs/44332836125) had successful baseline and eight model-evaluation jobs; the exact tested runner commit was \`17887db55c4fd1ec4a6a3512fb19370df291ea6d\`. Each new-model report records its prompts, outputs, numeric parser results, Python/package versions, runner digest and exact checkpoint SHA. The [machine-readable evidence summary](../../evidence/internal_reasoning/EXPERIMENTS_023_050_SUMMARY.json) contains the complete 28-ID metric table and all eight artifact checksums. Raw GitHub Actions artifacts are subject to 30-day retention unless separately archived.

The original EXP-001–022 study retains its reported limitations, including missing exact historical checkpoint revisions and in-sample baseline selection; a newer experiment run does not retroactively repair those gaps.

**Interpretation:** This is a small, exploratory, non-replicated experiment collection, **not** evidence of general mathematical intelligence, novel complexity lower bounds, a Millennium Prize proof, or production-ready mathematical verification. The 50 experiment IDs are not 50 statistically independent, externally reviewed studies.

### Scope and limitations

- Explicit small deterministic samples and non-instruction-tuned pretrained GPT-2 models.
- Greedy generation with 8 completion tokens and a strict initial-integer parser.
- No proof the arithmetic items were absent from pretraining data.
- No held-out independent confirmation, causal claim, generalization guarantee or external review.
- Other MathOS components are bounded research prototypes with their own separate evidence requirements.
