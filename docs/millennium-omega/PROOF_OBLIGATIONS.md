### Governance

1. Research records may be exploratory, conditional or falsified, but may not
   advertise MathOS certification of global Millennium theorems.
2. Evidence must identify its precise finite/numerical/conditional scope and
   the specific missing step to the full Clay target.
3. The TECT policy admits only RESEARCH_ONLY packets. Any claim to a global
   mathematical proof is rejected regardless of a matching SHA-256 digest.
4. Formal Lean/Coq Boolean policy examples are not proof artifacts for any
   of these seven mathematical obligations.
5. An independent, theorem-level proof and genuine external review are
   outside this software gate. Never infer them from green GitHub Actions.

### Poincare boundary

The accepted historical result belongs to Grigori Perelman's proof and external
mathematical recognition. MathOS does not claim an independently reconstructed
formal proof. This distinction is enforced by tests and packet auditing.
