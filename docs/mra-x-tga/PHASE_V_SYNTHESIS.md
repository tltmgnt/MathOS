## Algorithm
For eliminated set E, the remaining primal graph connects two remaining vertices exactly when they have an original graph path whose internal vertices lie in E. Eliminating one vertex creates a clique among its remaining neighbors, which establishes this invariant by induction. Thus the remaining separator depends on E rather than on the order used to reach E. The exact recurrence is F(E)=min over v outside E of [d_v product of d_u for u in its current separator + F(E union {v})], with F(V)=0. The finite recursion considers every possible next variable and reconstructs a minimum-cost permutation. It optimizes the dense-table kernel's enumeration count, not wall time, memory, treewidth, or the mathematical objective value. This is an informal argument, not a mechanized proof. There are 2^n subset states and n*2^(n-1) candidate transitions for n>0, with additional boundary computation and order storage. Exact planner limit is ten variables; beam fallback retains the previous nine-variable, domain-size <=3 envelope.
