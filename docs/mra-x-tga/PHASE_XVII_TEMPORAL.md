# Phase XVII — temporal computational intelligence

Run python tools/mra_x_temporal.py --output temporal-evidence.json --model temporal-model.json. The opt-in in-memory research engine compares Phase XV's greedy migration choice with the other distinct candidate elimination order. Training uses two counterfactual trajectories over the current edit and next two known training edits, reverting to the greedy policy after the first action. Labels are relative three-transaction enumeration savings. The model uses seven current/past-only features and five nearest neighbors. Depart from the baseline only if at least four neighbors have positive gains, median gain is at least 10%, and the fifth neighbor is within unit Euclidean distance. Features, horizon and thresholds are implementer-selected. Inference does not receive future edits. This is offline counterfactual policy learning, not full reinforcement learning or globally optimal trajectory search.

18 training streams (8/24 variables), 18 held-out streams (16/48 variables), ten transactions each, new seeds and three edit regimes across chains, cycles and overlapping ternary factors. 56 paired training rollouts, 336 checked shadow transitions, 180 checked training trajectory transitions, 360 checked held-out transitions (876 per campaign). Five controls per campaign. All checked traces matched fresh reference and the certificate checker; held-out objectives agreed. Of the 56 training comparisons, 14 alternate orders saved work, 37 lost and five tied. Five showed higher immediate enumeration but lower three-step total. The frozen learner departed from the greedy decision once in 180 held-out edits. 17 held-out streams tied and one lost; total 17,154 vs 16,962 (+192, +1.13%). Two campaigns reproduced all non-timing evidence and identical model JSON. Separate SQLite Phase XV equivalence audit matched action, work and optimum for all 180 baseline transactions.

Three randomized-order timing repetitions per policy for each of 18 held-out streams (108 timed runs per campaign). Includes in-memory planning, inference and execution, excludes verification, training, model loading and SQLite I/O. Geometric-mean candidate/baseline ratios 1.02562 and 1.02389, worst 1.30014 and 1.38728. Both NO_PROMOTION. Predeclared gate requires 10% reduction in enumeration and runtime and no more than 20% slowdown on any stream. No default changes, no claim of durable learner integration, external benchmarking, original RE-X integration or mechanized proof. The model is saved in fingerprinted JSON; history features live in memory, and model fingerprints are not authentication. Next investigate action-conditioned learning, richer edit locality and matched holdout evaluation before expanding the policy.
