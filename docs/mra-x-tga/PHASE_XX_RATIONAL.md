Score each distinct candidate as 4U + wF, where U is the immediate upper bound and F is full selected-order enumeration. The weight w is chosen offline from 0,1,2,4,8. This is a cheap surrogate for potential future work, not a calibrated prediction of future edits. An alternate must reduce the score by at least 5% and have an immediate bound no greater than 125% of the baseline bound. The bound is not a guarantee of relative actual work. No extra hypothetical solver executions during inference, at most two extra scalar scores per transaction, and no additional candidate orders. The existing enumeration budget remains enforced. No wall-time or total-memory limit. The fixed score family and thresholds are implementer choices.
