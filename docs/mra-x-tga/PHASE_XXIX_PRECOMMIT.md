# Phase XXIX — incremental precommit proof verification

Run python3 tools/mra_x_precommit_audit.py --directory precommit-audit (new directory). Opt-in subclass mra_x_precommit.Store retains exact, previously verified bucket receipts in process memory. The service schema, retention and historical read/export paths are unchanged. It independently reconstructs the full certificate using the Phase XXIV verifier, checks the prepared problem and snapshot correspondence, and reuses a receipt only when all local domains, factor multiset, projection and witness rows are exactly equal. Full terminal and witness checks still occur. The candidate and staged receipts become the live state only after SQLite commit. Stale writers are refused by the existing version CAS. On creation/reopen, the Phase XXVIII full checks are retained and an additional complete verification seeds the receipts; no disk receipt is trusted. These extra seed visits are not included in the edit-only work counts but their time is included in the timed batches. Receipts are mutable trusted memory, not authenticated proof objects. No new process memory, CPU, power-loss, hostile-input security or implementation-refinement guarantee.

Each of two campaigns: nine new-seed synthetic streams (chains, cycles, overlapping ternary factors; 8/32/64 initial variables), twelve edits each (108 states, 99 feasible, nine infeasible), checked by both Phase XXVIII and incremental stores; every optimum, full enumeration, actual solver visits, action and certificate fingerprint agreed, with fresh reference and historical retrieval checks. Each policy reopens once per stream. 14 controls including witness-only change with unchanged projection, five injected incorrect solver results, invalid domain shrink, SQL insert abort, stale writer, all-reused no-op, fresh receipt seed on reopen, retention and corrupted certificate digest. Two reports reproduce identical non-timing content and verify their evidence digests.

Per campaign solver visits 14685 in each arm; full precommit visits 39301 versus incremental fresh visits 13801 (-64.88%); 2519 reused buckets; precommit factor lookups 82413 vs 30980. Combined edit-only solver and checker enumeration 53986 vs 28486 (-47.23%). Counts exclude initialization, receipt seeding, reopen and audit verification. Three randomized-order repetitions of both policies across nine streams (54 timed batches per campaign): fresh SQLite creation, 12 committed edits, one reopen, serialization and verification included; reference audit and historical reads excluded. Geometric mean per-stream median incremental/baseline time ratio 1.113455 and 1.122202; worst 1.292420 and 1.324677. One of nine streams faster in each run. NO_PROMOTION: work reduction does not translate into measured end-to-end speed improvement. Full structural scanning, receipt-key construction, hashing and the extra initial/reopen seed remain costs; individual contributions have not been profiled. Independent replication, original RE-X integration, formal kernel proof, cold-cache/memory benchmarks and remote CI remain open. No default change.
