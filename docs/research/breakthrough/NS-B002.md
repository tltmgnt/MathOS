# Exact scalar-balance concentration and the missing dynamical constraint

**MathOS NS-B002 | Breakthrough cycle 2 | 24 November 2026 | Unreviewed technical note**

## Abstract

The previous NS-B001 result excludes a subcubic instantaneous enstrophy bound. This note constructs a smooth, divergence-free, time-dependent periodic path satisfying BOTH the exact kinetic-energy and enstrophy scalar balances of unforced Navier–Stokes, with finite integrated enstrophy but divergent critical spacetime norm and no integrable small-tail threshold of the type proposed in NS-B001. The path is not established as a solution of the momentum equation. The construction refutes a restricted scalar-balance inference, not Navier–Stokes global regularity.

## 1. Exact proof obligation

Target NS.critical_bound, route NS.B: derive the L⁴ in time, L⁶ in space continuation estimate for all admissible unforced periodic solutions. We challenge the intermediate claim that the scalar energy and enstrophy identities, combined with spatial solenoidality and smoothness before a finite time, suffice to establish this estimate. All spatial integrals use unnormalized volume on the fixed 2π-periodic torus.

## 2. Construction of the path

By NS-B001 there exists a smooth compactly supported divergence-free W on R³ with S(W)>0, obtained by cutting off a periodic vector potential of the negative of the explicit positive-production seed. For a field F denote kinetic energy K=∫|F|², enstrophy E=∫|curl F|², palinstrophy D=∫|grad curl F|² and integrated vortex stretching S=∫ω·(ω·grad)F. These constants are positive except that S need not be positive for a general field; for W it is positive by construction.

Fix viscosity ν>0 and set b=ν[D(W)+E(W)²/K(W)]/S(W)>0, F=bW. Because K,E,D scale quadratically and S cubically under amplitude multiplication, the resulting constants K₀,E₀,D₀,S₀ satisfy

S₀−νD₀=νE₀²/K₀.

Set a=2νE₀/K₀ and choose λ₀ large enough to fit the support of F after dilation strictly within one fundamental cell. For 0≤t<T=1/(2aλ₀²), define λ(t)=(λ₀⁻²−2at)⁻¹/² and u(t,x)=λ(t)F(λ(t)(x−x₀)), periodically extended. The path is smooth in time and space on this half-open interval, divergence-free and mean-zero. It is not asserted to obey the momentum PDE.

The four scaling identities are K(t)=K₀/λ, E(t)=E₀λ, D(t)=D₀λ³, S(t)=S₀λ³. Since λ'=aλ³, direct differentiation gives

K'=-aK₀λ=-2νE;

E'=aE₀λ³=2(S−νD).

Thus BOTH exact scalar Navier–Stokes balances hold on the path for all t<T. Also ∫₀ᵀE dt=E₀/(aλ₀), which is finite. The kinetic energy decreases to zero. This strengthens the energy-only obstruction to include the exact enstrophy identity.

## 3. Critical divergence and strain tails

The spatial L⁶ norm scales by λ^(1/2); hence

∫₀ᵗ||u||₆⁴ ds = (||F||₆⁴/a) log(λ(t)/λ₀),

which diverges as t approaches T. In contrast the integrated enstrophy remains finite.

For the strain-tail criterion of NS-B001 let g=|grad u| and C_S be a valid mean-zero torus Sobolev constant. At any fixed time, Hölder and Sobolev give S≤C_S²||g||_(3/2)D. Since S₀>νD₀, the invariant norm ||grad F||_(3/2) is strictly greater than ν/C_S². Monotone convergence therefore gives η>0 such that || |grad F| 1_{|grad F|>η} ||_(3/2)>ν/(2C_S²). Under the dilation the gradient amplitude scales as λ² and the L^(3/2) norm is invariant. Any threshold B(t) attaining the required small tail must have B(t)>ηλ(t)². Consequently ∫₀ᵀB dt diverges. This directly challenges the proposed inference from the scalar balances to an integrable critical threshold.

## 4. Mathematical consequence and boundaries

The path is a counterexample to the intermediate implication with precisely the hypotheses stated above. It is not a counterexample to the unforced Navier–Stokes conjecture, and does not construct singular solutions of any PDE. In particular the pressure and Leray-projected momentum dynamics have not been imposed. Tao's averaged Navier–Stokes result supplies a broader established example of why energy cancellation alone is insufficient; our construction addresses this particular scalar-balance route. Novelty relative to all prior work is not assessed.

The missing mechanism must use the actual evolution equation, not merely its two integrated scalar consequences. A next target is to derive a nontrivial restriction on concentration from the projected momentum residual or the structure of vortex stretching and pressure. A candidate estimate must exclude this explicit path without assuming the desired continuation bound.
