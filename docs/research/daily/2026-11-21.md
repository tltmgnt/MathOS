### Navier–Stokes · NS.B

For smooth periodic incompressible flow with positive viscosity ν and zero forcing, u=(A exp(−νk²t) sin(ky),0,0) solves the equation with constant pressure for integer k on the 2π-periodic torus. Its nonlinear term vanishes since the field is independent of x. Holding A fixed keeps initial kinetic energy fixed while the initial velocity-gradient norm grows linearly in k. ### Yang–Mills and mass gap · YM.continuum_mass

It has a strictly positive gap at every fixed spacing, yet that gap tends to zero. This isolates the need for a uniform physical lower bound. ### Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer · BSD.rank_refined

Count points on E: y²=x³−x at four good primes, including the point at infinity. Reproduce with standard Python:

```python
for p in (5, 7, 11, 13):
    points = 1 + sum((y*y - x**3 + x) % p == 0
                     for x in range(p) for y in range(p))
    print(p, points, p + 1 - points)
```

Observed point counts are 8, 8, 12, 8. The Frobenius traces are −2, 0, 0, 6. These determine four local factors. The analytic rank requires the order of vanishing of the global L-function at 1. Next, explain the zero trace at primes 3 modulo 4 by pairing x with −x.
