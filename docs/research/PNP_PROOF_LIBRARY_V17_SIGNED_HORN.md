## Method

Use the established renamable-Horn class, extending the previous standalone
Horn development probe (tests/independent_pnp_v18_horn_probe.py).
For each original non-tautological clause and distinct literal pair a,b,
impose the sign constraints NOT(a becomes positive AND b becomes positive).
Each constraint is binary in the variable-flip decisions; an SCC solver
proposes one assignment, then checks the constraints. This is the standard
renamable-Horn recognition algorithm, not theorem novelty. If recognition
fails, return unknown; a non-renamable CNF is not necessarily hard.

After applying the signs, each Horn premise has zero or one positive
literal. A body of arbitrary finite arity can imply its head or a conflict.
Queue-based forward chaining uses one visit per body occurrence, ignoring
duplicate heads. A separate ordinary Horn-subset route skips non-Horn
clauses and may refute a wider formula. Neither route returns SAT.

A certificate gives the signed renaming and original clause indices
in derivation order. The checker reconstructs each step without the SCC
or queue and emits signed original unit clauses, ending in empty clause.
All additions must pass RUP against the original formula and the
independent residual-clause verifier. A trace uses at most n+1 clauses,
not necessarily a minimum dependency core. Signed recognition is bounded
by pair generation, which can be quadratic in a clause width; no overall
linear runtime or unrestricted complexity claim. Lean formalizes the
abstract induction on conjunction bodies and Horn traces, not SCC
correctness, signed transport at the Python level, or the RUP program.

## Evaluation

Compare frozen v17 exact/subsumption composition, ordinary Horn subset,
and full signed Horn recognition on 72 fresh formulas: 24 each random
2-CNF, random 3-CNF and independently generated signed Horn. The latter
family is generated by sampling Horn clauses then applying one random
polarity vector per instance; no template is planted and no reference
label used. It is a restricted family and separately reported. Seed
17403 (signed Horn subgenerator 317403).
Reject exact normalized duplicates against upstream and prior splits,
including the v17.3 mixed formulas. No signed-orbit exclusion or external
blinding. The library is frozen; no target-derived admissions.

Equal cooperative search/reconstruction caps (4M ticks, 15s, 100k
cells), not a hard process ceiling; independent replay separately timed.
All fresh formulas have exhaustive reference labels. Positive audit
rechecks every credited proof, upstream provenance and summaries, not
negative search or timing observations. Recognition tests exhaustively
enumerate signs on small random cases. No automatic credit as learned
template reuse; these are separate algorithmic schema routes.
The implementation is based on main and does not require the still-open
v17.3 branch. No direct path-vs-Horn performance comparison is claimed.
