# RH-Ω Phase I: universal Li-positivity investigation

## Exact target, not yet proved

For actual nontrivial zeros rho of the Riemann zeta function, with
multiplicity and the correct convergence/symmetric limiting convention,

    lambda_n = sum_rho [ 1 - (1 - 1/rho)^n ].

Li's criterion states (under its mathematical prerequisites):

    RH <=> (lambda_n >= 0 for every integer n >= 1).

The bounded runner provides a floating closed-form sanity check for

    lambda_1 = 1 + EulerGamma/2 - log(4*pi)/2

and delegates to MathOS attempt 005's exact **synthetic** finite symmetric-zero
countermodel. Those fabricated zeros are NOT Riemann zeta zeros. Neither
sample supports a conclusion about the global all-n target.

## Open proof dependencies

1. Precisely define zeta and xi, analytical continuation and the actual
   nontrivial zeros and multiplicities.
2. Reconstruct Li's criterion with its full summation, normalization and
   convergence assumptions from a reviewed mathematical source.
3. Prove lambda_n >= 0 for every n, using a noncircular exact theorem.
   Testing a finite prefix, even with high precision, is insufficient.
4. Or use Weil's criterion: specify its full admissible test space,
   involution and topology, then establish canonical G1 (density),
   G2 (admissibility), G3 (sign-stability), G4 (arithmetic tails),
   G5P (positivity of the **whole** admissible family) and G5 (transfer).
5. Independently formalize all required prerequisites and the final
   implication with a pinned trusted kernel and explicit axiom audit.
   Outside expert review is a separate obligation.

sources and decisive falsification test. Adversarial review must check
