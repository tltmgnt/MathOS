## 2. Mathematical argument and method

In the finite diagonal model T_a=diag(1,e^(−a²),e^(−a)), take vacuum-free squared overlaps w=e^(−1/a) and 1−w. The slow contribution first dominates at n=ceil([1/a+log(1−e^(−1/a))]/[a−a²]) for 0<a≤1/2. Hence physical time a n equals [1/a+log(1−e^(−1/a))]/(1−a) plus a rounding error in [0,a). As a→0, the logarithmic correction tends to zero, so a n∼1/a→∞. Numerically evaluate with log1p for stability.
