## 2. Mathematical argument and method

Let μ be a positive probability measure supported in [0,s]∪[f,∞), where 0≤s<f, with low-window mass w∈(0,1). Define L(t)=∫_[0,s] e^(−Et)dμ and H(t)=∫_[f,∞) e^(−Et)dμ for t≥0. Since L(t)≥w e^(−st) and H(t)≤(1−w)e^(−ft), H(t)/L(t)≤((1−w)/w)e^(−(f−s)t). For w≤1/2 the low contribution dominates by t=log((1−w)/w)/(f−s); for w>1/2 it already dominates at zero and remains so. Endpoint point masses at s and f give equality, proving sharpness. Consequently a regulator-uniform positive lower bound on w and on f−s suffices for a uniform visibility time, without any upper cutoff on high energies.
