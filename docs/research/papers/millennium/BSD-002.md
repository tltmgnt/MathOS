## 2. Mathematical argument and method

For E_d:y²=x³−d²x at p=5, the traces for d=1,2 are −2,+2. The Frobenius trace in degree r satisfies s_0=2, s_1=a_5, s_r=a_5 s_{r−1}−5s_{r−2}. Replacing a_5 by −a_5 multiplies s_r by (−1)^r by induction. Hence every even-degree trace agrees; odd-degree traces are opposites (and differ when nonzero). Independently count points over F125 and F625 using monic moduli x³+x+1 and x⁴+2 over F5. The cubic has no root in F5. Every root of the quartic has multiplicative order 16 (its fourth power is 3, of order 4), and ord_16(5)=4; hence the quartic is irreducible. Enumerate square multiplicities and sum over all x, including infinity.
