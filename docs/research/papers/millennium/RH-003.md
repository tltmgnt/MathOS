## 2. Mathematical argument and method

Let S_m={±1,…,±m,±iε}, with ε=1/4, and use the ordinary square functional from RH-002. For nodes j² the Lagrange cardinal satisfies |ℓ_j(0)|=2(m!)²/[(m−j)!(m+j)!]≤2 by cancellation of the factors k²−j². At −ε² the absolute value increases by ∏_{k≠j}(1+ε²/k²). Since Σ_{k≥1}1/k²≤2 (compare the tail with ∫_1^∞x⁻² dx), the sum of all ε²/k² is at most 1/8. For nonnegative factors with sum s<1, ∏(1+x_k)≤Σ_{r≥0}s^r=1/(1−s), by expansion. Thus |ℓ_j(−ε²)|≤16/7 and the interpolation coefficient B=ε²Σ_j |ℓ_j(−ε²)|²/j²≤32/49<1 uniformly in m. RH-002's parity decomposition and weighted Cauchy–Schwarz now prove that the least degree with negative ordinary square sum is 2m+1 for every m≥1, at the fixed offset ε=1/4. The witness is t∏_{j=1}^m(t²−j²).
