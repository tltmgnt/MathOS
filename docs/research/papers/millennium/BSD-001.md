## 2. Mathematical argument and method

Both curves have good reduction at 5. Count affine solutions directly and include the point at infinity. The traces a5 are −2 and +2. The local inverse Euler polynomials are 1−a5 T+5T². The coefficient at 25 of the associated Euler series is a5²−5=−1, whereas the extension-field Frobenius trace is a5²−10=−6; these are distinct quantities. Independently enumerate all pairs over F25=F5[u]/(u²−2), using that 2 is a nonsquare modulo 5. The extension trace implies 25+1−(−6)=32 points on each curve.
