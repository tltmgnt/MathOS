## 2. Mathematical argument and method

Let U be the divergence-free periodic datum from NS-001, with mean stretching −1/4. Take u=−A U, A>0. Vorticity and its derivative scale by −A, so mean stretching becomes A³/4. For solenoidal Fourier modes, the mean of |∇ω_U|² is the sum of squared wave-number norms squared times mean mode energy. The five sine modes have |k|²=1,1,4,1,2 and unit amplitudes in orthogonal Fourier modes. Hence the mean palinstrophy is (1+1+16+1+4)/2=23/2. For smooth unforced Navier–Stokes with viscosity ν>0 the periodic vorticity equation gives (1/2)d⟨|ω|²⟩/dt=⟨ω·(ω·∇)u⟩−ν⟨|∇ω|²⟩. At t=0 this equals A³/4−(23/2)νA², positive iff A>46ν. The checker replays the prior stretching result and exact rational threshold.
