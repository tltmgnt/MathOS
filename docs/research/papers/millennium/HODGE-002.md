## 2. Mathematical argument and method

In the square-free rational cohomology ring let U multiply by H=Σ A_i and let D remove one factor from each square-free monomial and sum. In the orthonormal square-free basis D is adjoint to U. On subset degree k, DU−UD=(2m−2k)I, by counting add/remove operations. For k<m, ||Uv||²=||Dv||²+(2m−2k)||v||², so U is injective. Complementing subsets interchanges U and D, giving surjectivity of U from degree m to m+1. The primitive kernel has dimension binom(2m,m)−binom(2m,m+1), the Catalan number. Every product ∏(Ai−Aj) over a perfect matching is primitive because H is the sum of the matching’s pair sums, each annihilating its own difference. Restrict to noncrossing matchings on ordered vertices. In lexicographic order favoring the smaller vertex, the leading monomial chooses the left endpoint of each pair; its set determines the noncrossing matching uniquely by stack matching. Distinct leading monomials give linear independence, and the number of noncrossing matchings is Catalan (standard Dyck-word bijection). Thus these algebraic divisor products form a rational basis.
