## 2. Mathematical argument and method

For a smooth periodic solution set E=mean |ω|² and D=mean |∇ω|². Use the standard mean-zero periodic interpolation inequality ||ω||₄≤C||ω||₂^(1/4)||∇ω||₂^(3/4), with normalized norms and a valid constant C. Incompressibility gives ||∇u||₂=||ω||₂ by Fourier orthogonality. Hölder and the full vorticity energy identity imply E'/2≤C² E^(3/4)D^(3/4)−νD. Maximizing the right side over D≥0 gives (27/256)C⁸ E³/ν³. Equivalently x³−x⁴≤27/256 for x≥0, from the factorization 27/256−x³+x⁴=(x−3/4)²(x²+x/2+3/16). Hence E'≤K E³ with K=27 C⁸/(128ν³). Integration on smooth intervals yields E(t)≤E(0)/sqrt(1−2K E(0)²t) for 0≤t<1/(2K E(0)²). For u0=−A U from NS-002, E(0)=(9/2)A² by orthogonality. The estimate uses full nonlinear stretching, not a frozen Fourier ansatz.
