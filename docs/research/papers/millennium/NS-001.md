## 2. Mathematical argument and method

On the 2π-periodic torus let u=(sin y+sin z, sin 2x, w), w=sin x+sin(x+y). Its divergence is zero. Write a=∂x w, b=∂y w, C=cos 2x, D=cos y, Z=cos z. The vorticity is (b,Z−a,2C−D). Direct expansion yields ω·(ω·∇)u=−ab(2C+D)+bZ(6C−D). The Z term averages to zero. Here ab=cos x cos(x+y)+cos²(x+y); its pairing with C averages to zero, and its pairing with D averages to 1/4 by the product-to-sum identity. The normalized torus integral is therefore −1/4. The checker independently uses exact small dyadic Fourier convolutions and extracts the zero mode.
