## 2. Mathematical argument and method

For C(t)=w exp(−s t)+(1−w)exp(−f t), 0<w<1/2 and 0≤s<f, the equality time is log((1−w)/w)/(f−s). In a family with 0<δ≤f−s≤M, uniform bounded crossover time is equivalent to a uniform positive lower bound on w. Indeed the log ratio is bounded iff w is bounded away from zero, and the gap difference is bounded above and below. Take s=a, f=1, w=a^p/4 with 0<a≤1/4. For p>0 the crossover is p log(1/a)+log 4+o(1); for p=0 it tends to log 3. Numerically evaluate the equality time and check the two contributions agree.
