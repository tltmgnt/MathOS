## 1. Result and scope

Use the inherited nine-group normalized selector family, n=19m-7, L=Theta(m^3), arbitrary negative cross clauses of widths one through three, fixed one-hot/OR/pin/guard clauses. Fix a positive integer B independent of m. The following is a deterministic uniform sufficient condition for eventual exact cubic-stage coverage, including unique SAT.

For each group g represent its digit a by a weighted binary signature consisting of every raw selected cross-clause bit containing that digit, aligned by support and other digit values. For a support H containing g give every bit weight m^(3-|H|). Let d_g be weighted Hamming distance. Construct at most B centers per group by deterministic farthest-first traversal, starting at digit 0, using the smallest digit on ties and stopping when every distance to the nearest center is zero or B centers have been selected. Assign digits to the nearest center, using first-center tie breaking. Let r be the largest distance to the chosen center over all groups/digits, and eta=r/m^2.

There is a fixed original three-symbol single-tape witness program (depending only on B) correct on the entire family, with visited span O(m^3 polylog m). On any sequence of SAT inputs for which eta tends to zero, its runtime is o(m^9)=o(L^3). Equivalently, it suffices that each group admits a partition into at most B classes of weighted signature diameter o(m^2), even if the class membership is noncontiguous, the partition is not supplied, and the convergence is arbitrarily slow. A finite B is fixed for each such program. No arbitrary growing-B bound is asserted.

More generally the runtime promise can be stated directly in terms of the computed majority-quotient error Delta defined below: Delta tending to zero suffices. For sufficiently small positive Delta, a bound is

    O_B(m^7 polylog(m+2) +
         m^9 Delta^(1/6) (1+log(1/Delta))^a),

for a fixed implementation-dependent a. At Delta=0 the bound is O_B(m^7 polylog m); outside the small-error promise the algorithm remains exact and may take O_B(m^9) using the inherited solver. No unconditional exact-cubic result for arbitrary selectors is claimed.

## 2. Detecting an approximate quotient without advice

For each g and each nonempty support H containing g of width k<=3, the raw signature section has m^(k-1) bits. Its weight m^(3-k) makes the total section weight m^2, regardless of k. The group signature has total weight (1+8+28)m^2. Identical signatures have zero distance even for different digit labels; weighted Hamming is a pseudometric with the triangle inequality. The farthest-first partition has within-class diameter <=2r. The usual elementary packing argument proves the covering guarantee: if there were a partition into B classes of diameter <=D and the final maximum distance exceeded D, the B selected centers together with a digit farthest from them would be B+1 points at pairwise distance >D, contradicting the partition. If the algorithm stops early at zero radius the conclusion is immediate. Thus r<=D for any such partition, and the algorithm needs neither its classes nor D. In particular a fixed bounded number of approximately identical signature types is recognized without knowing a tolerance or convergence rate.

For every support H of width 1..3 write F_H(z)=1 if its exact raw negative clause is selected, zero otherwise. The original tuple is feasible iff all these bits at its projections are zero. They are RAW bits, not the previous closed local tables. Form a quotient constraint table Q_H on tuples of group-class labels: for each nonempty Cartesian class cell choose the majority F_H bit, choosing zero on equality. Define

    Delta_H = #{z in [0,m)^H: F_H(z) != Q_H(class(z))}/m^|H|,
    Delta = sum_H Delta_H.

All class cells in the quotient are nonempty because only actual center classes are kept. There are at most B^9 full class assignments and constantly many supports.

To bound Delta_H, sample a uniform original H tuple X, then independently resample each digit uniformly within its own class to obtain Y. X and Y are conditionally independent uniform tuples within their common class cell. The probability that a majority prediction differs from X is at most Pr[F_H(X)!=F_H(Y)]: conditional on a cell with fraction p of forbidden entries, min(p,1-p)<=2p(1-p). Change the coordinates one at a time from X to Y. Each intermediate tuple is uniform on the entire H product, since resampling within classes preserves the uniform marginal and is independent across coordinates. For the g-th change, conditional on its two digits a,a' in the same class, the other coordinates remain independent uniform. The disagreement probability is at most (2r/m^(3-|H|))/m^(|H|-1)=2r/m^2. A union bound over the at most three changes yields Delta_H<=2|H| eta. Summing over the fixed supports proves Delta=O(eta). No assumption that substituting all representatives directly preserves the original model set is made.

Preprocessing is sequential. Parse actual canonical pool bits, extract actual pins, retain the original raw selector. Enumerate signature records and sort by (group,digit,support,context) as in prior signature notes; O(m^3) records. Each center can be copied to bounded scratch and compared by aligned sequential scans with every row; even materializing all pairwise distances costs O(m^4 polylog m) bit time and O(m^3 polylog m) span, with a fixed number of sequential tapes. Farthest-first with B fixed needs only repeated scans of the nearest-distance array and the chosen center rows; tie decisions and weights use O(log m) bits. To construct quotient counts, enumerate all raw bits, use sequential scans of O(m) class labels per lookup if necessary, sort by support and class tuple and count. Enumerate all disagreement entries by the same scans. O_B(m^4 polylog m) bit time and O_B(m^3 polylog m) visited span suffice, including empty/full raw tables. The inherited fixed-track sweep simulation charges O(m^3 polylog m) physical moves per simulated bit step, and initial raw expansion O(m^6 polylog m), so preprocessing costs O_B(m^7 polylog m) physical transitions. There are no free indexed queries. All saved tables and labels fit the span.

## 3. Recover models lost by the quotient

The quotient alone is not a sound replacement. For each support H let

    E_H = {z: F_H(z)=0 and Q_H(class(z))=1}.

These are original allowed entries falsely declared forbidden by the quotient. Their density is at most Delta_H. For every nonempty E_H, generate a branch of the original normalized family by KEEPING every original clause and adding all negative cross clauses on H whose tuple is NOT in E_H (OR duplicate selections). The new constraint forces the projection to belong to E_H. This is actual original syntax, including unary supports, with unchanged n, L, fixed clauses and actual pins. Empty E_H needs no branch. Any witness found is an unchanged original witness. Any original model whose class assignment violates at least one quotient constraint must satisfy such a branch, because its raw projection has bit zero whereas the quotient bit is one. Branches may overlap.

Any five-group superset of H has allowed join density <=|E_H|/m^|H|<=Delta. Invoke the parent's fixed k=5 dyadic solver on this actual raw normalized branch, keeping saved outer data on disjoint stationary product components. Its time for positive five-join density rho is O(m^9 rho^(1/2)(1+log(1/rho))^a + m^6 polylog m), and for zero density it rejects in O(m^(13/2) polylog m). This is the previous local-log bound with e=13/2. For sufficiently small Delta the upper envelope on 0<rho<=Delta is O(Delta^(1/2)(1+log(1/Delta))^a); the function is increasing sufficiently near zero. There are at most 129 such branches (9+36+84). The sparse-branch coverage is exact, even when quotient satisfiability differs from original satisfiability. No removal of original constraints or assumption about uniqueness.

## 4. Quotient-permitted boxes: exact conditional expectation or sparse branch

Enumerate every full class assignment passing the quotient tests. It defines a product box D_0 x ... x D_8 of nonempty original digit classes. Let p=min_g |D_g|/m. With independent uniform choices from D_g let V be the number of violated original support bits, an integer between 0 and 129. Its expectation is the sum of the forbidden fractions in the projected class cells.

If E[V]<1, construct a satisfying tuple deterministically. For each group in order choose the digit in its current class minimizing the conditional expected number of violated bits given all earlier choices. The current expectation is the average over the candidate digits of their conditional expectations; hence the minimum does not increase it. Once all digits are fixed the expectation equals the nonnegative integer V and remains <1, so V=0. No randomized trial or unproved density-to-witness step is needed.

For a concrete sequential implementation, filter the selected original cross records once to those whose specified digits belong to this product box, using sequential class-label scans, O(m^4 polylog m) bit steps. For each candidate for the next group, scan these filtered lists, ignoring records inconsistent with the already fixed values or this candidate, and count surviving forbidden entries per support. Multiply the counts by the number of unrestricted completions outside their support, using products of the remaining class sizes, to obtain the exact numerator over the current product size. A constant number of groups and supports gives O(log m)-bit integer arithmetic. No denominator is zero. Compare fractions by cross multiplication. At most 9m candidate scans of O(m^3) records cost O(m^4 polylog m) bit time and O(m^3 polylog m) span, or O(m^7 polylog m) original moves after the charged sweep simulation. Return the resulting actual original witness, not a quotient representative.

If E[V]>=1, use the original formula restricted by unary exclusions to all nine box classes and call the same fixed k=5 solver. Why is this inexpensive on the promise? Every projected cell of a quotient-permitted box has Q_H=0, so every selected raw forbidden entry in that cell is a disagreement. Consequently its forbidden fraction is <=Delta_H/p^|H|<=Delta_H/p^3 (since p<=1). Sum to obtain 1<=E[V]<=Delta/p^3, so p<=Delta^(1/3). A five-group support containing a smallest class then has allowed join density at most p. The inherited local-log theorem bounds the time by O(m^9 Delta^(1/6)(1+log(1/Delta))^a + m^6 polylog m) for sufficiently small positive Delta, with zero-density cases as before. In particular the bound is independent of the unknown class-size distribution. Even very small classes and false-positive quotient cells are handled exactly. Delta=0 allows no such heavy box and no false-negative branches.

The union of the exception branches and the original satisfying tuples within quotient-permitted boxes is exactly the original model set: every model either fails some quotient support or passes every quotient support. Every branch only adds original constraints. Conditional expectation is used for witness production within a permitted box, not for enumerating all models. The algorithm can stop at the first witness. If all branches and boxes fail, the original is UNSAT; halt on blank. The construction is correct without the approximation promise.

## 5. Original-machine accounting and exact clock

There are a constant 129 exception supports and at most B^9 permitted boxes. For each generate the actual restricted selector by scanning the saved canonical raw bits and OR-ing the extra clause bits from sequentially sorted records. Even O(m^4 polylog m) bit time per branch is sufficient, with sweep cost O(m^7 polylog m). Run the inherited fixed k=5 solver (or conditional-expectation procedure) on a separate simulated component, following its physical head at constant block overhead, retaining the other stationary data. Use its stated visited-span bound, mark used cells, erase scratch and resume the fixed dispatcher. This uses a fixed finite product alphabet encoded in constant-width binary blocks over the original symbols 0,1,2. B is fixed in the control; no B^9 tracks are needed, only sequentially processed boxes and fixed reusable scratch. Read the returned n raw bits, or on failure its blank halt result, and on success reconstruct/copy the unchanged original witness to a fresh raw contiguous region, placing the actual halt head at its first bit. Per-bit shuttles O(m^4 polylog m) suffice. No extra alphabet or head, uncharged global movement, or external oracle is introduced.

Summing the branch bounds and preprocessing gives the displayed O_B runtime. For the zero-density rejecting branches, O(m^(13/2) polylog m) is dominated by m^7 polylog m. All stationary data and reusable solver scratch occupy O_B(m^3 polylog m). For Delta tending to zero the normalized time T/m^9 tends to zero, because x^(1/6)(1+log(1/x))^a tends to zero and m^7 polylog m=o(m^9). With L=Theta(m^3), the fixed finite description of length ell_B receives at least L^3/2^ell_B transitions at stage 3 ceil(log2 L) once eligible. Thus the exact cubic inclusion holds eventually, independent of how slowly the approximation error tends to zero. No explicit numerical threshold or compiled transition table. As a necessary condition for actual cubic misses, for each fixed B there exist positive constants eta_B, Delta_B and a sufficiently large m_B such that misses with m>=m_B have both computed eta>=eta_B and Delta>=Delta_B. This follows by uniform small-error bounds and a fixed clock coefficient, not a claim that such misses exist.

## 6. A scrambled dense proper-join unique control with persistent natural occupancy

Let h run through powers of two >=32, m=2h+1, M=4h. Digit zero is special; nonzero digit 1+2x+tau has label x in [0,h) and type tau in {0,1}. Call labels x<h/2 safe. Unlike the contiguous type classes in the parent occupancy control, types are interleaved in natural digit order. Use the nine-cycle of groups; select only negative binary cross clauses, precisely the complement of these allowed pairs:

* On cycle edges, allow (0,0), or two nonzero digits of opposite types and DIFFERENT labels. Mixed zero/nonzero is forbidden.
* On other pairs, allow every pair with a zero. For two nonzero digits, forbid both labels being unsafe, and also forbid equality of the two digits; allow the rest.

Every pair has some exclusion; the graph is K9. Cycle constraints force all zero/nonzero statuses to agree, and nonzero types would two-color an odd cycle. The all-zero tuple is therefore the sole model, with its inherited fixed extension for every pin pattern.

Every proper group subset induces a disjoint union of paths/isolated vertices on the cycle. Fix a two-coloring there and choose distinct safe labels: at least (h/2)(h/2-1)...(h/2-k+1) allowed k-tuples for k<=8. Thus every proper join has density bounded away from zero. On any support of width<=3 every prescribed digit has a local extension: if zero, use all zero; otherwise color its component to agree with its type and assign the remaining coordinates distinct safe labels, avoiding its own label. A cycle neighbor's allowed rows have zero row {0}, and nonzero rows of equal size h-1, all distinct (opposite types have disjoint sets, within type different labels exclude different digits). Both directional versions hold. Hence raw and closed signatures are incomparable for every two digits, both pruning modes retain all m values, and every directional maximum fanout on local supports of size<=3 is Omega(m), by choosing a proper coloring and distinct safe parent labels and varying the appended safe label. Thus W=Theta(m^9). The cycle pair has m distinct nonempty rows; on every noncycle pair the h safe nonzero rows are all distinct (they exclude their own digit only). Consequently the minimum context-conflict count K is Theta(m) in both endpoint orders. Every pair has a forbidden row of size Omega(m), including unsafe rows on a noncycle pair, so D=Theta(m). These are the prior full-width, high-fanout, high-conflict and dense-join controls in one family.

Natural-order multiscale occupancy now stays uniformly positive for EVERY proper projection test, not only pairs. For each dyadic t>=32 with t<=h/4, there are at least h/t-2>=h/(2t)=M/(8t) full intervals entirely inside the safe nonzero range [1,h]. Each such interval contains at least t/2>=16 distinct labels of each type. In a tile choosing only these intervals on every coordinate, for any proper support choose its cycle two-coloring and greedily choose distinct safe labels inside the corresponding intervals, avoiding at most seven previous labels. It passes all its induced constraints. Therefore nu_k(t)>=8^(-9) for k=2..8. For t>h/4, the number of blocks per coordinate M/t is <16; the all-zero tile passes all tests, so nu_k(t)>=16^(-9). These bounds apply to the parent's natural dyadic order and every sufficiently large dyadic t; thus no vanishing-occupancy scale in that order exists. This is not a claim about arbitrary alternative relabelings or stronger tests.

Nevertheless each group admits a five-class partition: {0}, and each type split into safe/unsafe. Within any such class the raw pair signature sections differ in at most two entries each (on cycle edges the excluded matched label changes; on noncycle edges only the equality exception may change, or the unsafe rows are identical). Unary and ternary sections are constant. Therefore weighted diameter <=16m, and farthest-first with B=5 has r<=16m and eta=O(1/m). In fact the majority quotient error is O(1/m) by the general lemma, irrespective of whether the computed clusters match these five types exactly. The new runtime bound gives O(m^(53/6) polylog m)=O(L^(53/18) polylog L) on this control, strict cubic slack. It escapes the preceding listed density/occupancy and structural sufficient conditions but is covered by approximate quotient recovery. Its all-zero witness is trivial, so it is a separation of sufficient conditions, NOT evidence of computational hardness.

## 8. Disposition

Persistent occupancy in the original digit order and dense proper joins no longer exhaust the structural frontier: bounded approximate signature-type complexity can be exploited without losing any witness in the quotient. Any actual unbounded exact cubic miss sequence must resist every fixed-B vanishing-error promise above. Next investigate approximation by more general relational structure (rather than bounded unary digit classes), growth of quotient complexity with m, or an exact-clock algorithm on the residual.
