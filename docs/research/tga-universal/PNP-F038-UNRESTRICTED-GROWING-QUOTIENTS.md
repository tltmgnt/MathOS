## 1. Result

Use the inherited nine-group normalized selector family, n=19m-7, L=Theta(m^3), selected negative cross clauses on one to three distinct groups and inherited fixed one-hot/OR/pin/guard extension. Fix a rational 0<alpha<1. Let B=max(1,floor(m^alpha)). Compute the parent's deterministic weighted-signature farthest-first partition with up to B classes per group, and its majority quotient Q_H on each support H. Write Delta for the sum of normalized Hamming errors of all 129 raw support tables. There is a single fixed deterministic original three-symbol one-tape witness machine, depending on alpha only, correct throughout this family, with visited span O(m^3 polylog m). On any sequence with Delta tending to zero, its runtime is o(m^9)=o(L^3), hence eventual inclusion at exact stage 3 ceil(log2 L). This includes sequences admitting growing partitions of up to B approximate signature types with maximum weighted diameter o(m^2). No fixed bound on the number of types is required.

More precisely, for sufficiently small positive Delta its time is bounded by

    O(m^7 polylog m + B^9 polylog m
      + m^9 [Delta^(1/2)(1+log(1/Delta))^a
              + ((B+1)/m)^(5/7) polylog m]).

The Delta term is interpreted as zero at Delta=0. The integer a and all hidden constants are fixed. The bound is not an exact numerical cutoff and does not imply a subcubic algorithm for arbitrary selectors. The critical improvement is that there are no B^9 separate original-scale sparse-box solver calls.

## 2. Quotient preprocessing with a growing number of centers

For each group g, the raw selected-clause signature of digit a has sections indexed by supports H containing g and other digits in H. Weight each bit by m^(3-|H|). Weighted Hamming distance is a pseudometric. The parent's farthest-first construction selects the least-index maximizer of the nearest-center distance until B centers or zero residual distance, assigns each digit to its closest center with first-center ties, and has maximum radius r no larger than the largest diameter of any partition into at most B classes (packing proof in parent). The parent's independent-within-class resampling and coordinate telescoping proof gives Delta <= 2 sum_H |H| r/m^2. Neither proof needs B constant.

The signature record stream has O(m^3) entries. To select each center on fixed sequential bit tapes, keep a sorted nearest-distance and label array of O(m log m) bits. Scan the chosen row against all rows in order, rewinding the O(m^2 log m)-bit center scratch and updating the array sequentially. Cost O(m^3 polylog m) per center, O(B m^3 polylog m)<=O(m^4 polylog m). Retain class labels and sizes; the full signature stream and original selector fit O(m^3 polylog m). Compute every class-cell forbidden count f_H(c) and size d_H(c)=product of constituent class sizes, by enumerating actual raw support bits, scanning class-label lists for membership if necessary, and sorting/merging records. Cost O(m^4 polylog m) bit time, O(m^3 polylog m) span, including explicit zero counts. Mark Q_H(c)=1 iff 2f_H(c)>d_H(c). Class cells have d_H(c)>0. The resulting counted quotient has O(B^3) records with O(log m)-bit fields, even if all cells are retained. Integer arithmetic for floor(m^alpha) uses fixed rational powers and polynomially bounded counters. Initial parsing, pin extraction and normalized tape conversion are inherited; the fixed-track physical sweep simulation charges O(m^7 polylog m) preprocessing transitions. No random-access lookups or growing control alphabet are assumed.

## 3. Aggregate mass replaces the enumeration of expensive branches

As in the parent, recover all original models rejected by the quotient through at most 129 exception branches. For each support H let E_H={z:F_H(z)=0,Q_H(class(z))=1}, where F_H is the original raw selected bit. Add actual negative clauses on H excluding every tuple outside E_H, while retaining every original clause, and call the fixed k=5 dyadic solver. Empty exceptions need no branch. The five-join density on a superset of H is at most Delta. The parent's local-log density bound charges O(m^9 Delta^(1/2)(1+log(1/Delta))^a + m^7 polylog m) total for this constant number of branches, including zero-density branches and construction. Any returned witness is an unchanged original witness. At Delta=0 no exception branch is needed.

For a quotient-permitted full class assignment c, let D(c) be the product of its nine digit classes, n_c=|D(c)|, and

    v_c = sum_H f_H(c_H)*n_c/d_H(c_H).

This is the integer number of original forbidden-support occurrences summed over all tuples of D(c), counting multiple violations. E[V|c]=v_c/n_c. Call c light if v_c<n_c and heavy otherwise. The disjoint boxes of all class assignments partition the original domain. For every permitted box each violated raw bit is a disagreement, because Q_H(c_H)=0. Counting original full-tuple completions of each disagreement gives the global inequality

    sum_{permitted c} v_c <= Delta*m^9.

If a light box exists, deterministic conditional expectation (parent proof) finds a genuine model within it: repeatedly choose the digit minimizing the conditional expected violation count, which cannot increase the expectation, and at the final tuple the expectation is a nonnegative integer less than one. The actual original records and class membership are used, not the quotient's approximate representatives. One such call costs O(m^7 polylog m) physical moves by the parent sequential scans. If no light box exists, then every permitted box is heavy, so the ENTIRE quotient-permitted tuple region has size

    A = sum_{permitted c} n_c <= sum_{permitted c} v_c <= Delta*m^9.

This is the key aggregate mass bound. It is valid even if all individual permitted boxes are tiny, and does not require that their original satisfying subsets are disjoint from exception branches beyond the stated class predicate. A large number of individually heavy boxes can be handled by one search.

## 4. Find a light box without repeated original-scale scans

Use the counted quotient itself as the input of a dyadic spatial frame traversal on class coordinates. Pad each class domain to M_B, the least power of two >=B, with unary exclusions on absent class labels. Each quotient cell record includes its support, coordinate values, count f and denominator d, and the majority bit. Include all nonempty cells, including zero-count cells. At a node with local side length t, retain exactly the records whose coordinate values lie in the node's intervals, subtract offsets, and carry their numerical payload unchanged. At a leaf all nine class indices are fixed. Reject padding, check all quotient bits and test sum_H f_H/d_H<1 using exact rational arithmetic; 129 is constant so products and sums have O(log m) bits. Every valid leaf has exactly one cell per support. A successful leaf returns its class tuple through the stack. No relation table of full class tuples is constructed.

This uses the parent's spatial frame dispatcher, with the SAME fixed encoded product alphabet and spatially disjoint reusable frame intervals. Payloads and counters now use O(log m) bits even at small scales. Capacity O(t^3 polylog m) per frame, child translation and copying by within-frame shuttles O(t^6 polylog m) per node, leaf evaluation polylog m. The global log is harmless here because B is polynomially sublinear in m. There are at most (M_B/t)^9 nodes at scale t; summing t^6 over dyadic scales with these counts gives O(B^9 polylog m), including failed leaves, pushes and pops. The occupied interval along a path has O(B^3 polylog m) blocks. The root's actual class lists and payload counts are constructed once and kept on stationary components. No per-node original-scale access is made. The local class digits are reconstructed through saved child bits; any global digit-class membership lookup is performed only after the final leaf succeeds, using saved labels in the original-scale preprocessing span. No assumption of an efficient general quotient SAT algorithm is needed. If the traversal finds a light box, do the one conditional-expectation call and return. Otherwise all permitted boxes are heavy and the aggregate mass bound applies.

## 5. One augmented, class-contiguous search

If no light box is found, add to the original raw cross bits every forbidden quotient cell: select a negative clause on H whenever F_H(z)=1 OR Q_H(class(z))=1. Keep fixed clauses and pins. This augmented formula has exactly the original solutions whose class assignment is permitted. Together with the exception branches it covers every original solution, with no spurious solution. Reorder each group's digits by class index, then by original digit (contiguous classes); apply the resulting permutations to cross records only. Regenerate the fixed one-hot/OR/pin/guard extension in the normalized layout. This bijectively maps latent tuples, not literal auxiliary bits: after obtaining a local satisfying tuple, invert the digit permutations and reconstruct the original one-hot and prefix-OR bits and original actual pins. The original n,L are unchanged. Sequential membership scans, sorting, explicit canonical selector construction and permutation maps cost O(m^4 polylog m) bit time and O(m^3 polylog m) span, hence O(m^7 polylog m) original moves, including initial preparation and inverse witness recovery.

Run the parent's k=5 dyadic pruning solver ONCE on this augmented relabeled selector. Set M to the least power of two >=m. At dyadic scale t dividing M, a passing tile cannot have a coordinate wholly in padding. Call an interval exceptional if it meets a class boundary or the padding boundary, meaning it is not entirely contained in a single nonempty original class (exclude wholly padded intervals). At most B+1 intervals per coordinate are exceptional. Every nonexceptional passing tile is wholly within one permitted class box: otherwise a forbidden quotient cell on some support H of size <=3 rejects all assignments of H in that tile, and a five-group superset test must reject. Thus, if A denotes the total permitted mass,

    N_5(t)*t^9 <= A + 9(B+1)*t*M^8,
    nu_5(t) <= Delta + 9(B+1)*t/M.

The bound counts all projection-passing tiles, not only visited tiles. It does not assert that arbitrary passing tiles contain global models. In the parent's scale-split theorem e=13/2, so with a fixed log exponent a' the time for the augmented search is

    O(m^9 [Delta + (B+1)t/M + t^(-5/2)(1+log(t+2))^a']
        + m^6 polylog m).

For analysis choose dyadic t=Theta((M/(B+1))^(2/7)); on the fixed-alpha promise this tends to infinity and lies between 2 and M eventually. Boundary and tail terms balance at O(((B+1)/m)^(5/7) polylog m). The solver need not compute this analytical scale. This proves the main bound after adding the exception and quotient traversal costs, absorbing Delta into the Delta^(1/2) term for small Delta. Unlike a naive B^9-fold original-scale search, the only B^9 cost is on compact quotient payloads.

All subroutines are invoked on fixed reusable product components, following their local physical head with constant overhead per physical simulated transition, preserving outer records, marking visited extents and clearing them before reuse. Root parsing, local raw selector copies, final raw n-bit witness output, return to its first bit and failure blank halt are charged by the inherited O(m^7 polylog m) preprocessing and O(m^4 polylog m) output bounds. The spatial frame stack uses a fixed finite alphabet/control, not an input-dependent recursive track alphabet. The fixed machine can decide alpha's power bound with binary arithmetic, and can run the same exact procedure outside the vanishing-error promise (the augmented solver has the inherited unconditional O(m^9) bound). Its description length ell is fixed. Since alpha<1, B^9 polylog m=o(m^9), the boundary term tends to zero, and the error term tends to zero whenever Delta does. Consequently the clock 2^(3 ceil(log2 L)-ell)>=L^3/2^ell eventually dominates this time. No numerical threshold, general exact cubic coverage, or hardness claim.

## 6. Strict separation from fixed-type approximations

Let q=2^d grow, m=4q^2+1, and use the nine-cycle as in the parent unique control. Digit zero is special. Nonzero digit 1+2l+tau has type tau in {0,1}, label l in [0,2q^2), and class vector c(l)=0 for even l, or floor(l/2) mod q for odd l, identified with a d-bit vector. On cycle edges allow (0,0), or two nonzero digits of opposite types and distinct labels. On other pairs allow all pairs involving zero, and two nonzero digits iff their class vectors have binary inner product zero and the two digits differ. Select exactly the complements as negative binary clauses. Every pair has forbidden entries, and the odd cycle forces the unique global solution all zero. This is an easy control, not hardness.

There are at most 2q+1 types (zero and the type/vector pairs). Within a type/vector class each pair signature differs in at most two entries due to label or digit equality exceptions. Thus weighted diameter <=16m, and farthest-first with B=floor(m^(2/3)) eventually covers with r<=16m. The majority error is O(1/m), so the new theorem applies. An explicit approximate relation removes the equality exceptions and has O(m) raw disagreements over all 36 pairs. The majority error of this explicit partition is no larger; the farthest-first guarantee follows separately. Here Delta^(1/2)=O(m^(-1/2)) and the boundary term for alpha=2/3 is O(m^(-5/21) polylog m); the latter dominates, yielding the conservative normalized runtime O(L^(184/63) polylog L).

For any fixed B0, even an arbitrary partition into at most B0 classes cannot achieve vanishing quotient error. Consider the noncycle pair (0,2) and the 2q^2 row digits with odd label l, distributed equally among q vectors (2q digits each). Rows with different vectors differ on at least q^2-2 columns: restrict columns to odd labels, where half the q vectors distinguish their dot products and each vector has 2q digits; at most two equality exceptions alter this count. A quotient prediction supplies at most B0 distinct expanded row vectors, regardless of the column partition. For any such predicted row, at most one vector class can have a row within distance strictly less than (q^2-2)/2, by the triangle inequality. Thus at most 2q B0 odd-label rows can be that close to any prediction. The total normalized pair disagreement is at least

    (2q^2-2q B0)*(q^2-2)/(2 m^2),

which tends to 1/16. This lower bound applies to every bounded unary partition, not merely farthest-first. It is a representation lower bound, NOT a witness-search lower bound.

For context, the family also satisfies the earlier structural residual tests: all proper joins have constant-order density by two-coloring the induced paths and choosing distinct even labels (all vector zero). Every local specified digit extends on supports of size <=3 by assigning other digits distinct even labels with compatible types. A cycle neighbor gives distinct equal-size nonzero rows and the incomparable singleton zero row, hence full static/contextual widths. Directional max fanout is Omega(m) by choosing even labels and appropriate types, so W=Theta(m^9). Each cycle pair has m distinct nonempty rows, and noncycle pairs have at least 2q^2 distinct even-label rows (each excludes its own digit only); hence K=Theta(m). Every pair has a forbidden row of size Omega(m), using nonzero class vectors for noncycle pairs, so D=Theta(m). Natural-order dyadic occupancy stays uniformly positive: for t>=64 and t<=M/8, there are at least M/(4t) whole intervals inside the nonzero range, and each contains at least 15 distinct even labels of either type. Any choice of these intervals for the nine groups admits each proper-support projection by type-coloring its induced paths and choosing distinct even labels. Hence nu_k(t)>=4^(-9); at larger scales the all-zero tile gives nu_k(t)>=8^(-9). These are theoretical separation properties; the regression below specifically checks approximation errors and row separation, not every earlier parameter.
