## 2. Canonical cover and exact certification

Use the rooted eight-child dyadic tree of the three-dimensional padded support array. A cube is full when all its entries are selected forbidden bits. A maximal full cube is full and either is the root or has a nonfull parent. All selected forbidden bits belong to a unique maximal full cube: ascend from the singleton until the last full ancestor. Distinct maximal full cubes are disjoint, since aligned dyadic cubes in the same tree are nested or disjoint. Their union is exactly the original selected set, and their number is at most the number of selected singleton bits.

Build the cover without a random-access oracle. For each selected singleton emit its support key and every ancestor path/scale key with unit count. Sorting and reducing identical keys gives the exact number of selected bits in each occupied cube. Mark full iff the count equals t^3 (including padded positions in the volume; padding has zero bits). Emit queries for the parent keys of full cubes, sort/merge against marked counts, and retain a full cube precisely when the parent is absent or nonfull, or the cube is root. A constant number of sequential sorts over O(m^3 log m) records of O(log m) bits suffices across the fixed set of supports. Time and span O(m^3 polylog m) sequential bit steps, O(m^6 polylog m) original physical steps by the inherited fixed-track sweep. The original actual raw bit table is kept as well for map generation and guard building.

For a local aligned cube D, each maximal cover cube intersects D only by containment in one direction. If a cover cube contains D then the clipped cover is the singleton {D}; no other cover cube meets D. Otherwise all intersections are whole maximal cubes contained in D. Thus, to certify all C_B(D), emit each maximal cube's ancestor keys (including its own), sort and count. These counts equal C_B(D) at each key emitted. Cubes with missing keys either lie strictly inside an already-full cube, giving count one, or have no forbidden entries, giving zero; both satisfy the certificate. For p=5/2 check n^2<=c0^2*t^5, for p=2 check n<=c0*t^2. A fixed rational exponent permits a fixed-power integer comparison. No need to enumerate all empty cubes. Certification fits O(m^6 polylog m) original time and O(m^3 polylog m) span.

For comparison, every clipped cube contains at least one selected singleton in D, so C_B(D) is no larger than the original selected tuple count in D. The new p=5/2,c0=2 test therefore accepts every choice passing the parent's t^(5/2) singleton cap. It can also accept dense arrays with many forbidden points represented by a few cubes.

Use the parent's actual raw row distance counts to determine active wide supports, and test the certificate for each of the 504 choices g,K. Constantly many support combinations. Among eligible choices select the smallest full-average score by the inherited greedy policy and pair-sliced generator. The sharper mixed-rate greedy comparison gives

    sigma <= 38 beta_* (1+ln(1/beta_*)) + 1/m + delta_*.

The choice, policy and score are computed, not given. Original raw parsing, pin extraction, cover construction, distance tables, certification and selection together fit O(m^7 polylog m) physical time and original-scale span. If no eligible choice exists, use the inherited exhaustive original-record worker (unpruned spatial DFS), keeping correctness without asserting a strict saving there.

## 5. Local frame transfer with exact cube clipping

At a positive full node of side t, enumerate its 512 full child vectors. The six P bits determine the projected child header in the stationary tree. A zero header skips the child. Otherwise, for each residual record in the active parent frame intersect its locally aligned dyadic cube with the child projection on that record's support. Clip as above, translate the starts by the child interval offsets and retain its side length (or the child side length if clipped from a larger cube). The resulting record starts and side fields use O(log(t+2)) bits; in the child they are recoded with local child field widths. Since child projections are aligned, a surviving parent cube gives at most one child record. The union of the child records is exactly the restriction of the parent's forbidden relation to that child, by the distributivity of intersection over union. Inductively it is the actual original residual relation on the child. No requirement that a residual record's source support be entirely projected or entirely private. The relative position of an intersection within a local frame is computed with fixed integer arithmetic. Per-record clipping comparisons and translation require only a polynomial in local log(t+2) on fixed components.

The child frame cap follows from the GLOBAL canonical cover's clipped count at that child: repeated clipping is associative, nonempty pieces of different original disjoint cubes never merge or duplicate, and a single ancestor cube still contributes only one clipped piece. Hence a local record list always has O(t^p) records, even after many recursive transfers. At a singleton, every nonempty residual record is a violated constraint, so a true guard flag and an empty list characterize an original restricted satisfying tuple. One may also reject early if a residual cube fills its entire support projection, but this is optional and not used for the bound.

The inherited stationary physical navigation uses relative delimiters within the parent subtree of extent O(t^6 polylog(t+2)). Copy only the clipped residual records to the appropriate reserved child frame by per-bit shuttles with local counters. Private alternatives reuse the same frame sequentially after clearing; active ancestors occupy different projected depths. Charge O(t^p) source records for each of the constant 512 child choices, each with at most the parent subtree extent times local logarithms in charged movement. Including child navigation, filtering, clearing, pop/return and success propagation, local work is

    O(t^(6+p) (1+log(t+2))^A).

Local records need no global offsets or global log m fields. Outer coarse offsets are restored only at the root. At successful leaf recover original latent digits and actual one-hot/OR/guard/pin extension, emit contiguous n raw bits and halt with physical head at the first bit. The once-only O(m^4 polylog m) output work is dominated. Halt on blank if the search is exhausted.     sum_t min(M^9*t^(p-3), R*M^3*t^(p+3))

## 7. Dense hierarchical separation from the singleton residual cap

Modify the parent's finite-field slow-loss control. Let m=q=2^d, with digits all binary vectors, also identified with GF(q), and unary zero exclusions in every group. Set g=0,K={1,2,3},P={0,1,2,3,4,5}. Keep the main triples {0,i,j}, 1<=i<j<=3, forbidding the binary dot product <x0,xi+xj>=1; keep all inner-product-one binary forbiddances among groups 1..8 and equality forbiddances on pairs involving 0. Keep the wide field-hash support {0,4,5} from the parent, F(a,u,v)=1 iff the first l bits of a*u+v in GF(q) are zero, where l grows arbitrarily slowly and zeta=2^-l. On triples among groups 1..8 except {6,7,8} keep the sparse forbidden Latin relation x+y=z (vector XOR). On {6,7,8} replace the Latin relation with

    F(x,y,z)=1 iff w=x XOR y XOR z is nonzero
                     and the index of its highest set bit is even,

with zero-based bit indices. This is a ternary relation on all three private coordinates. Its exact whole-domain forbidden density is sum_(even j<d) 2^j/q, since each XOR value has q^2 preimages. Along even d it tends to 1/3 and along odd d to 2/3; both allowed and forbidden densities are bounded below by a positive constant. Therefore neither the singleton forbidden count nor a minority-polarity singleton representation has a subcubic whole-cube count. The prior residual count certificate fails on this private support for every fixed p<3 and fixed constant at sufficiently large q.

Nonetheless the canonical dyadic cover satisfies C_B(D)<=2t^2 for EVERY aligned local cube D of side t. In D the bits above log2(t) of each digit are fixed. If their XOR is nonzero the highest set bit of w is already determined, so the whole cube is homogeneous and needs at most one forbidden cube record. Otherwise the low bits vary; at each next dyadic split, four of the eight child combinations have next XOR bit one and are homogeneous with status determined by that bit's parity, and four have next XOR bit zero and may recurse. At depth i there are at most 4^i unresolved nodes. Summing the maximum number 4^(i+1) of emitted homogeneous-one cubes per level up to log2(t)-1 gives at most (4/3)(t^2-1)<2t^2. The all-zero XOR leaves are allowed. Maximalization can only reduce record count; by the clipping lemma this bounds the actual local canonical cover. The Latin residual supports have at most t^2 singleton records by fixing their first two digits. Hence the new p=2,c0=2 certificate holds, including for this dense hierarchical residual, without a sparse allowed projection or minority-polarity count.

The representative and loss calculation for the parent's control is unchanged because the modified support does not contain g. Isolate zero and the three context digits, and select one representative for each nonempty remaining fiber of the three dot products with conditioning digits. At most 12 representatives, narrow loss <=5/m, and wide loss <=zeta(1-zeta). For any distinct field elements a,b the map (u,v)->(a*u+v,b*u+v) is bijective, giving exactly q^2*zeta(1-zeta) directed wide loss. The context-free row union density tends to one. Choose l=max(1,floor(log2(log2(d+2)))) eventually; then zeta->0 but zeta*(log m)^B diverges for every fixed B>0. The new structural promise holds while the designated prior mixed-rate promise fails. The tuple with groups 1..8 all equal to vector 3 satisfies the non-g constraints: self inner product zero, XOR of two copies zero not 3 for Latin forbiddances, and XOR of three copies equals 3 with highest bit index one (odd) for the modified constraint. Choose nonzero a distinct from 3 such that the hash of a*3+3 is not zero in all its first l bits. Multiplication by 3 is bijective in GF(q), so such a exists for sufficiently large q. This is an easy SAT control, not unique or hard.

For any alternative g,K the parent's lower bounds still hold because the modification does not remove any of the raw supports used there: if g=0 and K differs from {1,2,3}, a main triple with one free other group gives q^2/4 weighted loss on distinct nonzero digits when its other group is conditioned, or q^2/4 when both are free; nonzero-to-zero loses m^2 on its unary bit. If g>=1 a free binary neighbor among groups 1..8 gives mq/4 weighted loss on distinct nonzero digits, with the same unary obstruction to zero. Apart from the zero source and selected representatives, the loss is bounded below by a positive constant after normalization. Thus for sublinear capacity no alternative coordinate choice has vanishing full average loss. This separates the new certified structural promise from the preceding raw-singleton certificate even when arbitrary choices are permitted. It is not a separation from every previous algorithm or a complexity lower bound.

## 9. Disposition

The previous residual record-volume hypothesis is replaced by a dyadic cover complexity cap. Dense forbidden and allowed residual relations can now satisfy the arbitrary-slow full-average recovery promise at original-scale space when their homogeneous forbidden regions have a sufficiently economical dyadic description. Arbitrary dense residual arrays with no such compression remain open, as does unconditional exact cubic coverage, unbounded B_3, general SAT and P versus NP resolution. Next investigate a transfer architecture that does not require sparse or dyadically compressible residual representations, or a stronger certified restriction based on actual local allowed guard fibers.
