## Sound necessary summaries

Fix a checked affine parameterization and supplied binary clause tree. The exact upward relation U_v on S_v comprises boundary functionals that extend to satisfying assignments of the subtree's leaf predicates. For a finite set P, aff(P) denotes its affine hull (empty if P is empty). Represent a hull by a consistent linear equation system on the ambient parameter coordinates whose rows belong to S_v, or an explicit false sentinel. Choose an integer threshold h. At every leaf and every subtree with direction rank dim W_v <= h, enumerate local functional signatures on W_v, evaluate all predicates in the subtree and take the affine hull of their projected signatures on S_v. Otherwise compute H_v by intersecting the two child hull cylinders and projecting onto S_v. This requires Gaussian elimination alone. The local rank bound at leaves is ell, so preprocessing costs 2^max(h,ell) poly(L), including independent certificate replay. Projection of a consistent linear system onto S_v is obtained by restricting its fixed functional to the intersection of its coefficient row space with S_v, as proved in earlier work.

Induction gives U_v subset H_v: the exact small-subtree computation is a superset by definition, and at a large join every feasible extension satisfies both child hulls, hence their projected conjunction. Empty H_v certifies infeasibility; a nonempty H_v is not a feasibility certificate. At low rank, H_v is the exact affine hull, not necessarily the exact relation. At large rank, it can be larger than the exact affine hull due to correlation loss.

## Certified fiber filtering

At a queried internal node, let K=S_a+S_b and fix its signature z on S_v subset K. Instead of enumerating all 2^(dim K-dim S_v) extensions, combine the equations for z with those of H_a and H_b. All equation rows belong to K. If inconsistent, reject the entire fiber. Otherwise let r be the rank of the combined system. Exactly 2^(dim K-r) functional extensions to K survive, and every genuinely feasible extension survives. Enumerate this reduced fiber by finding one ambient solution and projecting a basis of the homogeneous kernel onto K; enumerate the resulting independent signature shifts. This is polynomial preprocessing per visited state, not global assignment enumeration. Test candidate child signatures recursively, memoizing state answers for the fixed predicates only. A positive answer reconstructs a full functional from the chosen leaf restrictions and verifies it. A negative answer exhausts the reduced fiber or proves it inconsistent. No rejected region need be tested point by point.

The verifier regenerates the summaries and the reduced fibers in a fixed order, validates each memoized entry after its child dependencies, and checks trial counts and the final witness. A failed or missing certificate is not a negative result. For visited states V and inspected surviving candidates q, total time and replay cost are 2^max(h,ell) poly(L) + poly(L)(1+|V|+sum q), including preprocessing. The whole algorithm is polynomial if the threshold is logarithmic and the actual query exploration polynomial. There is no universal guarantee of that second condition. The implementation handles one root query with fixed leaf predicates (original or one complemented candidate); it is not a full repeated semantic repair compiler.

## Exact improvement on both sign families

The earlier sign family has m shared bits x_i, left pairs (l_i OR u_i),(l_i OR not u_i) and right anchors (x_i OR v_i); l_i is uniformly x_i or not x_i. Pair the two left clauses, group the left pairs, and join with the right anchors. The entire tree width is max(m,2), while the two root-child boundary ranks are exactly m. With h=4, each rank-two left pair has exact hull forcing the required x_i value; joining the groups preserves those pins. The right hull is unconstrained. The combined root fiber has exactly one candidate in either polarity, instead of 2^m unfiltered candidates. Each descendant with a supplied compatible signature has at most a constant number of remaining local trials in the supplied tree. Total preprocessing, search and verification are polynomial, independently of the polarity that previously forced exponential root trials. This is a structural improvement for this explicit family, not a lower bound against other algorithms.

## Exponential residual fiber even with sound hulls

For m disjoint triples (a_i,b_i,c_i), the left half enforces NAE per triple using the positive and negative triples. The right half enforces a_i=b_i=c_i, encoding each equality of endpoints x,y with four width-three clauses: (not x OR y OR u),(not x OR y OR not u),(x OR not y OR v),(x OR not y OR not v), with fresh private u,v for each equality. No complete parity class occurs on any scope; no affine source equations are extracted. Pair all clauses for each equality in a rank-four subtree, group the two equalities per triple, and group the triples in each half. The root child separator spaces both contain exactly the 3m shared variables. For h=4 the left per-triple exact affine hull is the full cube (the six NAE points span it affinely). The left half hull is therefore full even when a subtree is handled by exact enumeration. The right rank-four equality gadgets yield exact affine equality hulls; merging them preserves all equalities. The right half hull contains exactly 2^m signatures, each triple either 000 or 111. Hence the root fiber after certified filtering still contains 2^m candidates, all incompatible with the exact left relation. For this fixed search algorithm and tree, the root exhausts all of them. Memoization does not avoid those distinct root trials. This is an unconditional exponential lower bound for this particular affine-hull filtering policy, not for arbitrary exact summary algorithms, other decompositions or SAT runtime. In particular a method detecting the local contradiction between each NAE and equality block could immediately reject this easy family. No universal barrier to logical simplification is asserted.
