## 2. Simultaneous cover and a sufficient rate-free condition

For an original generated map and fixed H, let L_H,t(a,D) be the number of assignments with c in aligned context cube D of side t and nonzero I_H(a,p_c(a)). Let J_H,t(a,D) be the sum of the largest floor(C*t^alpha) frequencies of these class identifiers. Define

    tau_alpha,C = (1/m^4) sum_H sum_(t<=B dyadic) sum_(a,D)
                       (L_H,t(a,D)-J_H,t(a,D)).

At any trimming step the surviving multiset is a submultiset of the original nonzero class multiset at that key. Retaining the original top classes would discard at most the original tail count; retaining the current top classes discards no more. Sum over all levels and all supports. Later deletions preserve previously attained caps. Therefore gamma<=min(1,tau_alpha,C) for the class-trimming candidate. This proof does not assume independent support errors or disjoint deletion sets. It counts an actual heavy assignment only once in gamma. No claim of optimal simultaneous trimming, and the upper bound can exceed one before clipping.

For a sequence of generated maps with beta,delta_n,delta_w->0 and

    lim_(C->infinity dyadic) limsup_(m->infinity) tau_alpha,C(m) = 0,

the selected Q_alpha->0. Fix a sufficiently large dyadic C making the limsup tail arbitrarily small; eventually it passes the production cap, and at that FIXED C, C^P lambda<=C^P delta_w->0. The minimum is no larger. This is rate-free, not an interchange of an unbounded C with the limit. A particularly simple case is a fixed constant C0 bounding every nonzero directed class image by C0*t^alpha on all aligned context cubes, separately for each a,H; then no trimming is required at a fixed C>=C0. It is also enough for all cap violations to occur on a vanishing fraction of source/context assignments that the trimming actually deletes.

The support separation is important. On a t-cube it may be that the joint tuple of class identifiers over two supports has t^2 distinct values even though each support has only t. No joint product of class identifiers is needed in the recursive worker, because each wide exception is searched separately. A simple exact control is a target b=1+c1+4*c2 on a 4-by-4-by-4 context cube for source zero, with two supports whose nonzero differences depend respectively on (b-1) mod 4 and floor((b-1)/4). Each support has four rows at the root and two in each side-two child; the joint signature and actual label have sixteen at root. At C=1,alpha=1 no independent class trimming is needed, whereas raw-label root trimming retains at most a quarter. This is a prescribed-map component example, not a claim about the actual adaptive greedy policy.

## 7. Strict score separation on actual generated maps, with all raw rows distinct

Take m=2^d, r=floor(9d/10), q=2^r and T=[m/2,m/2+q) (for sufficiently large d, q<=m/2). Let f(c) be m/2 plus the r-bit string obtained by interleaving successive low bits of c1,c2 (even output positions from c1, odd from c2). It ranges uniformly over T in every aligned context cube of side B=2^floor(2d/3) for large d: all input feature positions are below log2 B and independent. The actual target is nonzero. Use g=0,K={1,2,3}. Put a unary support on g forbidding a outside T, and two binary supports {g,1},{g,2} forbidding mismatch with their respective interleaved output positions. Together they force the unique digit f(c). Optional unary zero exclusions on all other groups. All g-containing narrow bits vanish at f(c). No other cross supports except the following wide H={0,4,5}. This is an easy SAT family: choose nonzero contexts, a=f(c), nonzero x and y>=m/2, and other digits nonzero. No hardness or complete primal graph claim.

Let j=m/2^l, l positive integer tending arbitrarily slowly to infinity, with 4<=l<d eventually. Set zeta=j/m. For free coordinates (x,y) define three disjoint column regions:

    A: 0<=y<j,
    B_mid: m/4<=y<m/2,
    D_high: m/2<=y<m.

Let h_a(x,y) be the binary dot product of the full d-bit labels a,x XOR the low bit of y. For a outside T set raw forbidden row F_H(a) to one on all B_mid, to h_a on D_high, and zero elsewhere. For b in T set F_H(b) to one on A, to h_b on B_mid, and zero elsewhere. Every raw row is distinct: within either type the h patterns differ for distinct labels, and across types A distinguishes them. This separation therefore does not rely on merging equal raw rows.

For each a outside T and EVERY b in T, the directed difference is exactly the common indicator of A, since old source is one everywhere in B_mid and target is zero on D_high. It has D0=m*j=m^2*zeta ones. For two distinct sources outside T the directed loss is D_s=m^2/8; for distinct sources in T it is D_t=m^2/16. Indeed over x uniform and y in the corresponding half/quarter interval, (h_a,h_b) is uniform on two bits for a!=b: the distinct nonzero linear forms with shared coefficient one on the independent low y bit are independent. Thus both probabilities are 1/4. Target-to-outside loss is nonnegative, and such substitutions also incur unary narrow loss m^2. The all-zero self difference holds as usual.

We prove the actual unpenalized full-average facility greedy selects a singleton f(c) in every context. The summed wide loss to any target in T is the same,

    (m-q)*D0 + (q-1)*D_t.

The unique target f(c) incurs zero narrow loss from every source. Every other target in T incurs positive narrow loss from source f(c). A candidate outside T has summed wide loss from other outside sources alone at least (m-q-1)*D_s; since q/m->0 and zeta->0 this eventually exceeds the summed loss to f(c). Hence f(c) uniquely minimizes the first greedy round. After this selection an additional target in T can improve only its own source (other outside sources still lose D0, other inside sources still lose D_t, narrow loss cannot improve below zero). Gain <=D_t<m^2. An outside candidate can improve only its own source by at most D0: other outside sources lose D_s>=D0, and inside sources incur unary loss m^2>D_t. Thus every extra marginal is below the facility cost m^2; future marginal gains are nonincreasing by the minimum-residual rule. The actual optimal positive greedy round is exactly C_c={f(c)}, p_c(a)=f(c) for every a. Consequently

    beta=1/m, delta_n=0,
    delta_w=(1-q/m)*zeta+(q-1)/(16m).

For arbitrary maps on this designated configuration every distinct replacement of a source outside T incurs wide loss at least D0. Self fraction <=beta. Hence delta_w >= max(0,1-q/m-beta)*zeta. The loss is genuinely capable of arbitrarily slow decay under vanishing capacity. For example l(d)=max(4,floor(log2(log2(d+2)))) eventually yields no fixed polynomial decay. This lower bound concerns average loss, not SAT time.

For the new class worker at C=1,alpha=1, all assignments for sources outside T have exactly one nonzero class across every context cube, so none are trimmed. Any deletions for sources inside T affect at most q/m of all assignments. Therefore gamma<=q/m and lambda<=delta_w, giving

    Q_1 <= 1/m + q/m + delta_w ->0.

In contrast, for every source outside T, the actual replacement labels uniformly cover q distinct nonself digits in each B-sized context cube. In the previous raw-label trim at its largest permitted exponent and capacity, the number of labels retained is at most m^(1/32)*B^(5/4) <= m^(83/96). The ratio of this bound to q tends to zero because 83/96<9/10. Thus the raw-label trimming discards a 1-o(1) fraction of assignments for every outside source at the first scale, uniformly over both exponent options and all allowed capacities. Since outside sources have fraction 1-q/m->1, the previous score cannot vanish on these generated maps. The separation holds even though all raw rows are distinct and the retained directed loss is positive. It is deliberately stated as a separation of computed sufficient conditions on the designated actual generated maps, not from the entire prior adaptive solver over other compressed groups or map policies. Indeed the unary support excludes the outside sources from actual witnesses, and this easy family can be covered by older mechanisms. No lower bound on general SAT or P versus NP conclusion.

## 9. Disposition

Source-specific directed-difference classes replace raw target diversity without losing exact wide recovery. Independent support caps avoid the artificial product of class identifiers; null rows need no capacity. The original raw-label candidate in the portfolio provides genuine score dominance without an unjustified greedy dominance assertion. The distinct-raw-row construction separates the local score conditions even with slow positive wide loss on the designated greedy maps. However global difference classes may all be distinct on a support, and equality can fail even for rows differing in only a few columns. Next investigate free-square-local difference equivalence or a controlled approximate class cover with exact sparse corrections and a rate-free physical charge. General arbitrary-slow full-average recovery, unconditional exact cubic coverage, unbounded B_3, general SAT and P versus NP resolution remain OPEN.
