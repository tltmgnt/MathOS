## Exact symbolic projection by translation saturation

Use the provenance-checked affine parameterization x=x0+Gt and the supplied clause tree of the preceding work. Fix an order of the ambient parameter bits. Represent a message on a direction space S by a reduced ordered binary decision diagram (ROBDD) for its cylinder, a Boolean function of the ambient parameter t that depends only on its linear functional restricted to S. The two terminals are false and true; a nonterminal queries one bit, with distinct low/high children later in the order. Intern equal triples (bit,low,high), and suppress equal children. The representation is canonical for the fixed order. Leaf predicates are compiled from their at most 2^ell permitted signatures (ell<=3 for width-three CNF) using parity tests. Each parity test has at most two states per position.

For any ambient function f and output direction space T, its exact cylindrical projection is

  Pi_T(f)(t) = OR_{v in T^perp} f(t XOR v).

This follows directly from equality of restrictions to T. Compute a basis v_1,...,v_d of the orthogonal complement by Gaussian elimination, then iterate f_j(t)=f_{j-1}(t) OR f_{j-1}(t XOR v_j), starting at f_0=f. Induction shows that the accumulated shifts range over the span of the processed vectors. The final result is therefore exact without listing 2^d shifts. In an ROBDD, a translation swaps the children at every queried bit in its support and recursively translates those children. It preserves the fixed order. Boolean AND and OR are computed by the usual recursive apply algorithm with memoization of operand pairs. The join of two message cylinders is their conjunction followed by Pi_T. The previous affine gluing proof justifies the same upward and downward message recurrences; no independence assumption about the factors is made.

For local projections, intersect an outside message with each allowed leaf signature (a conjunction of parity pins), testing the result for nonemptiness. Compile the earlier exact translation predicates Q_i and do the final upward pass. To reconstruct a direction, recursively intersect the two child messages with a pin to the desired parent boundary signature, choose one satisfying ambient vector from the ROBDD, and pass its child signatures downward. At leaves choose compatible local signatures; solve their combined equations once. The same gluing theorem guarantees consistency. Check the resulting direction against every Q_i. Failure of translation synthesis is not an UNSAT decision for the source.

## Resource accounting and certificate scope

Let M be the total number of distinct ROBDD nodes allocated by the manager over all operations for one candidate, including temporary apply and shift results, local projection tests, and witness reconstruction. For a supplied tree and order, the algorithm uses polynomially many symbolic operations in L and 2^ell: O(n) translation saturations per projection, and polynomially many parity tests, applies and leaf enumerations. Memoized apply uses at most the product of the two operand diagram sizes per call; shifts and diagram traversal are linear in the diagram size. Hence total time and storage are poly(L,M,2^ell), counting intermediate allocations rather than only final roots. This is not a promise of polynomial M. A deterministic transcript of allocated nodes, inside/outside roots, leaf projections, translation predicates and translation roots is checkable by replaying the exact symbolic recurrence from the source, without trusting the producer's witness. The checker uses the same fixed variable order and includes its own temporary computation. It is not a polynomial-time verifier relative solely to the original input on arbitrary instances. The code implements one designated occurrence and the natural ambient bit order; arbitrary orders can be introduced by permuting variables/directions consistently, but automated order discovery is not supplied. The ordinary 3-CNF leaf rank is bounded; high rank can occur at inner cuts.

## Connected high-rank NAE chain

Take m triples (x_i,y_i,x_(i+1)), each constrained by NAE (positive and negative triples); the variable order is x_1,y_1,x_2,y_2,...,x_m,y_m,x_(m+1). Duplicate the entire chain across two halves of a supplied tree, and pair the two clauses of each triple in siblings. The specified extraction leaves every clause residual (the positive and negative patterns are not complete parity classes). The half boundary has rank 2m+1. Every triple direction scope overlaps its neighbor, so the coordinate-scope grouping of the preceding factored algorithm joins them into one group when projecting the completed half onto its full scope. The exact chain relation projects onto the independent NAE triples with odd indices: these have disjoint variable sets, and each intervening even triple extends arbitrary endpoint values by a suitable y. Hence the projection is the product of ceil(m/2) NAE triples. Since projection of an affine flat is affine, any union-of-flats representation of the half relation needs at least ceil((3/2)^ceil(m/2)) pieces by the earlier bound. This lower bound concerns a single union for that connected group, not every possible factored representation.

In contrast, in the displayed variable order, the chain is recognized by a finite-state scan storing at most the preceding two bits, together with rejection. Therefore its ROBDD has O(m) nodes. Each message in the two-pass algorithm is a cylindrical projection of a conjunction of a subset of chain-local predicates (possibly with one modified leaf). For the duplicate tree, every direction scope and separator is a coordinate subspace. A projection onto coordinate bits amounts to existentially forgetting other bits. The finite-state scan for arbitrary local triple predicates can treat forgotten bits nondeterministically, then track a subset of its constant-size state set, so projected diagrams also have O(m) nodes in this order. Duplicate predicates at the same triple are simply intersected. Adding fresh c and the clause (not x_1 OR c) at the end couples the first and last bit, but storing x_1 adds only a constant factor. Translation predicates are again on the same local scopes. During an individual join, the conjunction of two projected diagrams has polynomial size by apply; successive coordinate projections are projections of the underlying local conjunction. Witness reconstruction pins coordinate boundary bits, which are unary restrictions and preserve the same scan argument. Thus all actual operations on this supplied family allocate polynomially many nodes, including intermediate results. There is a nonempty bad relation for the new candidate, and flipping c repairs it. This is a new tractable architecture example, not a claim that the chain is difficult for other SAT solvers.

## All-orders OBDD obstruction

Let G be the k by k square grid and F_G=AND_{uv in E(G)}(x_u OR x_v). No binary parity gadget is complete, so the chosen extraction leaves all clauses residual. Duplicate the formula in the two halves of a supplied tree; the half boundary consists of all k^2 coordinate directions. Its exact feasibility message is F_G. For every variable order, cut after floor(k^2/2) variables. If at least k/4 rows contain both sides, each mixed row supplies a crossing horizontal edge. Otherwise both a pure prefix row and a pure suffix row exist (each side contains at least floor(k^2/2) vertices, more than the mixed rows can contain for k>=3); every column then supplies a crossing vertical edge. Thus there are Omega(k) crossing edges. The degree is at most four. Greedily keep one crossing edge and discard crossing edges with endpoints equal or adjacent to either endpoint. At most 2(4+1)4=40 edges are discarded per selection, yielding an induced matching of Omega(k) edges across the cut.

For every subset of its prefix endpoints, assign exactly those prefix variables zero and all other prefix variables one. The chosen zeros are independent, and no prefix clause is falsified. Two different subsets give distinct residual functions on the suffix: choose the suffix endpoint of a differing edge, set it zero and every other suffix variable one. By induced matching it is adjacent to no other chosen prefix endpoint. The full formula holds precisely when its matched prefix endpoint is one. Hence 2^Omega(k) distinct residual functions must be represented by distinct reached nodes or terminals of every ROBDD in that order. Every order therefore needs 2^Omega(sqrt(n)) nodes for this exact message, n=k^2. This is a representation barrier, not a SAT time lower bound; the formula is trivially satisfiable, and the earlier repair elimination removes every monotone clause. It applies to the fixed explicit ambient coordinate representation, not arbitrary invertible affine reparameterizations, other diagrams, or altered decompositions.
