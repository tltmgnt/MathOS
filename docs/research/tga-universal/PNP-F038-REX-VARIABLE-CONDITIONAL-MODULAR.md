## Explicit limits and polynomial accounting

Default caps: 4096 attempted probes and 200000000 charged dense-size units, both configurable nonnegative safe integers. Before each prime call on n component coordinates and m rows including assumptions, charge (n+1)(m+1)(n+m+1) using exact BigInt comparison. No call starts if the remaining cap is insufficient. The counter is a size proxy, not a literal operation count, and excludes source discovery, reconstruction, nested inference, certificate checking and fallback. A probe may count as attempted even if its next prime call does not fit. Only completed contradiction records are retained. Partial views remain exact; partial sound records can establish a terminal contradiction. No saturation claim when budget-stopped.

For k original coordinates and r integer rows, each component with n coordinates has at most 1+2n+4 binom(n,2)=1+2n^2 probes (the constant component at most one). Across disjoint nonempty components the sum is O(k^2+1). Four primes are fixed. Per prime D7O dense elimination costs O(mn(m+n)+m(m+n)) field operations plus O(mnp) residue testing, with O(m(m+n)) working storage; m<=r+2. Thus a conservative complete unbudgeted probe-stage bound is O((k^2+1)(r+2)(k+1)(k+r+2)) fixed-field operations, plus inherited polynomial source discovery and nested inference. At most 2k^2 distinct non-tautological unit/binary consequences; each record stores O(r+2) references, so O(k^2(r+2)) record words plus shared source table. Independent checking costs are polynomial in explicit proof and input size. General SAT and end-to-end physical bounds do not follow.

## New source-language limitation: exact-one integer equations

For k=31 take scopes {i,i+2,i+5} mod k for every i, and for k=127 take {i,i+1,i+7} mod k. Each factor allows exactly 100,010,001. Every coordinate occurs in exactly three factors, each requiring sum of its bits equal one. Summing the k equations gives 3*sum x_i=k, impossible since both k are 1 mod 3. Equivalently, the integer rows sum to 0=1 mod 3. This is a direct family-wide counting contradiction for any such regular exact-one encoding with k not divisible by three.

But D7O/D7P integer source extraction deliberately accepts even tight balance equations and local parity of width at most two, not arbitrary local integer-affine identities. On these exact-one factors the local GF(2) row has width three; it is insufficient to translate it as an integer equality solely from parity. Actual predecessor affine elimination extracts all k local rows and reaches wide-fallback (5 free coordinates at k31,7 at k127), preserving the original residual. D7P discovers zero usable integer rows, zero components, zero probes and returns UNKNOWN, regardless of its assumption budget. D7N discovery has k local proof blocks but none of the accepted integer source rules. The inherited tree independently refutes both: k31,4774 charged units,84 nodes; k127,18266 units,141 nodes. This is a source-language limitation of modular inference, not a full-pipeline obstruction.
