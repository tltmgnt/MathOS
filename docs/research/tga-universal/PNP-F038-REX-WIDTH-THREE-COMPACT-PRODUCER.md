## Mechanism and preconditions
The inherited raw normalizer validates the header and every selector, constructs 130 packed mask blocks, preserves consumed selector prefix and maintains five binary counter lanes plus group words. The existing bound emitter and trimming code remain unchanged. Only after successful full validation and bound emission, replace the old loadBlock0 destination with compactStart. Let B be the blank separating mask zero from consumed selector prefix, A the blank between selector and workspace, h the old stream delimiter. Initially the interval to the right of B contains binary selector prefix, A, binary workspace, h, and contiguous bound stream followed by blank. Construct two one-cell placeholders (0 blank 0 blank) at B+1..B+4; B+4 becomes the new stream delimiter. Mark B+5 as the new write frontier. Transfer each bound bit from the old bound stream using a temporary blank marker and finite-control bit value. Each shuttle follows exactly the three intervening blank-separated regions, writes the bit at the new frontier and advances the frontier one cell, then restores the source bit. On completion erase old bound, old workspace and unused raw tail by three reverse binary-region scans, stopping at the frontier; cross the relocated bound to its new delimiter. There are no new logical tapes, alphabet symbols, or unbounded host operations.

For genuine m>=2 the validated selector prefix has length at least the unary/pair/triple selector pool, in particular grows at least cubically in m; the bound stream has length 2*bit_length(m-1), after trimming. It fits with the five placeholder/frontier cells well inside the prefix. The raw tail and original workspace remain nonempty during each relocation shuttle. At each transfer boundary the new bound prefix is the copied prefix of the old bound; the frontier is its first blank; the remaining raw tail, workspace and source suffix remain binary and delimited. The temporary source marker is unique within the bound. Thus the next bit is copied exactly, and the source marker restored. At completion the three reverse erasures remove only the former source and unused workspace/raw tail. The new delimiter, bound and every packed mask cell survive. All cells to the right of the new bound are blank. The two placeholders reproduce the old loader's two intervening separators; hence the original j+2/j+3 mask shuttle counts and canonical field order remain valid. Induction over existing loader copies gives identical canonical stream. Bridge uses frames=0 and consumes that stream at the new delimiter. Worker/projector unchanged. External witness semantics require the halt-head witness and blank termination, not an absolute head coordinate or preservation of the spent raw prefix. D6U intentionally destroys the validated selector prefix after success and changes output location. It is not a substitute when the full old checkpoint must be retained; the original producer remains available. Before successful validation, existing refusal paths are unchanged. Equivalence claim is for genuine normalized input and inherited normalization behavior, not an unrestricted proof on arbitrary malformed tape layouts.

## Conditional resource refinement
Let L be original raw length, P packed-mask extent, Bn number of emitted bound bits, S canonical stream length. Relocation takes O((L+Bn)*Bn+L) transitions, since each bound-bit shuttle traverses at most O(L+Bn) cells, with a linear erasure sweep. Bn=O(log m). The subsequent loader never crosses the long consumed selector prefix: source extent P plus current stream length at most S plus constant separators bounds each copy shuttle. With at most O(S) emitted tokens and fixed130 field scans, loader cost O((P+S+1)(S+1)). Previously the source-to-stream shuttle included the raw region, giving O((L+P+S+1)(S+1)). This is a source-level phase bound, not an explicit universal numeric resource certificate. Normalization, mask deposition, bridge and D6T worker costs are unchanged. P,S=O(L) for genuine normalized layouts; the inherited conditional O(L^2(p+1+V)) end-to-end envelope and admitted exponent 2+(h+b)/3 are unchanged. No unrestricted SAT reduction or universal cubic guarantee. Fixed-nine explicit normalized inputs already admit polynomial exhaustive enumeration.
