## 1. Result and scope

Use the inherited nine-group normalized selector family, n=19m-7, L=Theta(m^3), raw forbidden cross-clause bits F_H on supports of size one through three and the fixed one-hot/OR/pin/guard extension. Choose one compressed group g and three conditioning groups K disjoint from it. For a full context c on K, source digit a and representative b define the nonnegative weighted loss on H containing g:

    d_H,c(a,b)=m^(2-|H minus ({g} union K)|)
                * #{z on H minus ({g} union K):
                     F_H(a,c,z)=0, F_H(b,c,z)=1}.

Omit unused conditioning digits from the raw lookup. Call H wide if |H union K|=6; equivalently H is a triple containing g and neither other digit belongs to K. All remaining H containing g are narrow (|H union K|<=5). Use the hybrid distance

    D_c(a,b)=sum_(H narrow) d_H,c(a,b)
             +m^2 * 1{some wide H has d_H,c(a,b)>0}.

For nonempty contextual sets C_c and maps p_c(a) in C_c put

    beta=sum_c |C_c|/m^4,
    gamma_w=#{(a,c):some wide H has d_H,c(a,p_c(a))>0}/m^4,
    delta_n=sum_c sum_a sum_(H narrow) d_H,c(a,p_c(a))/m^6.

Each distance section has total weight m^2, and there are m^4 source/context pairs. The total normalized hybrid score is sigma=beta+gamma_w+delta_n.

There is ONE fixed deterministic original three-symbol single-tape witness program, correct throughout the nine-group family, that takes o(m^9)=o(L^3) transitions along any sequence admitting beta, gamma_w, delta_n all tending to zero for some g,K and maps. It needs no supplied maps, choice of coordinates or convergence rate. More precisely its computed score sigma yields, for sufficiently small positive sigma,

    O(m^(42/5) polylog(m+2) +
      m^9 sigma^(1/5) (1+log(1/sigma))^a)

physical transitions and O(m^3 polylog m) visited span, for a fixed integer a. It follows that the exact cubic stage eventually includes a witness on the promise. Outside the promise the procedure remains exact with O(m^9) time (additive stage slack only). This replaces the previous three-context requirement of zero loss on almost all source/context pairs across ALL supports by that requirement only on potentially width-six supports. Arbitrarily slowly vanishing average loss is allowed on all other supports. This does NOT solve arbitrary width-six average-loss recovery.

In particular, full three-context average-loss recovery is obtained on the structural subfamily in which every selected ternary involving g meets K outside g (or all wide sections are independent of g). More generally, for a competing map for which every positive wide total loss is at least lambda*m^2 for a fixed lambda>0, gamma_w is at most the normalized wide average loss divided by lambda. No such loss gap is assumed for arbitrary selectors.

## 2. Hybrid exact witness reduction

Select a map minimizing D_c per source digit, using fixed tie breaking. For a chosen representative set, this can only decrease the combined loss relative to any supplied competing map. Let P={g} union K, U={(a,c): some wide H has positive loss under the map}, S={(a,c): a in C_c}, and A=S union U. Since D_c(a,a)=0 and losses are nonnegative, every selected representative has zero minimum distance, so S and U are disjoint for the minimizing map. A has normalized density beta+gamma_w. For every narrow H define Q=H union K and the allowed exception list

    E_H={z on Q: F_H(z_H)=0,
                   F_H(z_H with g replaced by p_(z_K)(z_g))=1}.

The sum over narrow H of |E_H|/m^|Q| equals delta_n: for each H and c,a, the free other digits number m^s with s=|H minus ({g} union K)|; the loss weight is m^(2-s), so dividing the sum by m^6 is exactly dividing the count by m^(4+s). Here |Q|=4+s. All E_H have width four or five. No width-six relation is enumerated.

Every branch keeps every original constraint and additionally requires membership on its support in A or E_H. For any original solution x, apply its contextual replacement to g only. If the replacement succeeds, the resulting original solution has projection in S. If it fails and a wide support was newly violated, the original source/context pair lies in U and x is retained in A. If it fails only on narrow supports, x belongs to some E_H. These are exhaustive possibilities. Conversely every branch model is an original model. Thus the reduction is exact for satisfiability/witness recovery, not necessarily equality of the whole original model set. Under unique SAT a successful substitution returns the same model. Branches may overlap, and there are at most 38 total branches. Every branch support includes K, a fact used for amortized generation below.

## 3. Adaptive hybrid facility selection

There are 37 raw sections containing g, each with weighted size m^2. The hybrid distance is in [0,37m^2] because a nonzero wide indicator replaces one or more wide sections. It is not required to be symmetric or metric. In a fixed context start with residual losses R_a=37m^2. Add one unused candidate b at a time minimizing sum_a min(R_a,D_c(a,b)), ties by smallest digit, and update residuals and minimizing labels (assign labels in round one even on ties to the initial bound). Choose the smallest positive k minimizing k*m^2+F_k, F_k=sum_a R_a. Compare all 504 choices g,K and use the least aggregate score, fixed tie break. Keep only the best choice and score in the first pass.

For any competitor of size h in a context and minimum loss F_*, the sum of marginal gains from its members is at least F-F_* at any state of residual total F. The greedy gain is at least (F-F_*)/h when this is positive, and monotonicity handles the other case. Thus F_k<=F_*+37m^3 exp(-k/h). Taking k=ceil(sqrt(h*m)) and averaging over m^3 contexts, using concavity of square root and exp(-x)<=1/x, gives

    sigma <= 38 sqrt(beta_*) + 1/m + gamma_w,* + delta_n,*.

The right-hand loss can be measured using any competing map into its sets, because the distance minimum cannot be larger. In particular the procedure detects the stated vanishing promise without knowing it. For a fixed maximum h of representatives per context with competitor loss O(1/m) after normalization, taking k=ceil(h log m) yields sigma=O(log m/m).

## 6. Local-log amortization and the exact clock

For any branch with R allowed projected tuples on k=4 or 5 groups, let rho=R/m^k. The number of passing full nodes at scale t is bounded by min((M/t)^9,R*(M/t)^(9-k)). Visited descendants are bounded by a fixed multiple because every visited nonroot has a passing parent and there are 512 child choices. Charge mandatory coarse roots separately by O(m^9/b polylog m), within the extraction bound. Empty restrictions have no descendants. For R>=1 extend the descendant sum to all dyadic t through M:

    sum_t min(M^9/t,R*M^(9-k)*t^(k-1))*(1+log(t+2))^a.

At crossover t0=M/R^(1/k), the two polynomial terms meet. On both sides the appropriate term decays geometrically away from t0, and local log growth on the upper side is bounded by a fixed polynomial in the dyadic distance from the crossover. Hence the sum is O(m^9 rho^(1/k)(1+log(1/rho))^a), with no global log m factor. Every branch density is <=sigma. For small sigma the upper envelope of this expression is O(m^9 sigma^(1/5)(1+log(1/sigma))^a) by monotonicity sufficiently near zero. All branch counts are constant. Combining the global preparation bounds gives the displayed theorem. The unconditional unpruned dyadic sum O(m^9 sum_t t^(-1)*(1+log(t+2))^a)=O(m^9) also holds.

The greedy bound ensures sigma tends to zero on the promised sequence, so runtime divided by L^3 tends to zero. The actual fixed program description of length ell receives at least L^3/2^ell transitions at stage 3 ceil(log2 L) once eligible. The little-o bound eventually fits that allocation, without computing ell. This is conditional exact-cubic coverage; no numerical threshold, compiled original-machine table or actual U execution. ## 7. Strict separation from the preceding zero-cover and two-context promises

Let q=2^d, m=q-1, digits corresponding to distinct nonzero vectors in GF(2)^d. Use the preceding three-context algebraic control: forbid <x0,xi+xj>=1 for triples with 1<=i<j<=3; forbid <x0,x1> != <x1,xj> for j=4..8; forbid inner product one for pairs among groups 1..8. ADD binary disequality on EVERY pair of the nine groups (union with existing forbidden pair bits). No other constraints. This graph is complete. For d>=18 choose nine weight-two vectors with disjoint supports: all distinct and pairwise orthogonal, so a satisfying tuple exists. Easy SAT control, not unique or hard.

With g=0,K={1,2,3}, every ternary containing g meets K, so there is no wide loss. For each context, isolate each distinct conditioning digit in its own singleton; on the remaining digits choose one representative per nonempty fiber of (<a,x1>,<a,x2>,<a,x3>). This uses at most 11 representatives. It preserves every ternary involving g, and introduces no newly forbidden conditioned binary inequality: nonconditioning source digits map outside the three fixed digits; fixed digits map to themselves. The five unconditioned binary disequalities can each newly forbid at most one partner digit, of weight m. Thus gamma_w=0, beta<=11/m and delta_n<=5/m. The adaptive score is O(log m/m) by the sharper greedy bound; the conservative runtime is O(m^(44/5) polylog m)=O(L^(44/15) polylog L).

For any three-context choice for g=0, at least one unconditioned binary disequality neighbor exists, so every distinct replacement has positive loss. For any g>=1 and any three conditioning groups there is an unconditioned neighbor among 1..8 distinct from g. For distinct nonzero vectors a,b, the orthogonality plus inequality relation has at least q/4-1 partner digits x satisfying <a,x>=0, <b,x>=1 and x!=a (subtract at most one). This is positive for q>=8. Thus EVERY group and three-context choice admits only self coverage at zero total directed loss, and the previous zero-cover score beta+gamma equals 1 for any sets. The new hybrid condition is strictly more permissive.

For any two-context choice for g=0 the prior main-triple argument remains: at least one of groups 1,2,3 is free, and a triple containing it yields loss at least m*(q/4-1) if its other non-g digit is fixed, or q(q-2)/4 if both free. For g>=1 an unconditioned binary neighbor among 1..8 gives loss at least m*(q/4-1). These raw section losses are unaffected by other selected supports. Thus every two-context distinct replacement has loss at least a constant times m^2 for large m. Sublinear representative capacity implies normalized average loss bounded below, just as in the earlier separation. The control distinguishes these specific sufficient conditions, not all prior algorithms or witness-search lower bounds.

## 9. Disposition

Three-context average-loss recovery now works on all narrow sections while wide sections use the projected positive-loss flag. The hybrid theorem strictly extends the previous all-support zero-loss envelope and the two-context average-loss promise in the demonstrated representation control. It still requires vanishing wide projected loss; a tiny positive width-six loss for almost every source/context pair is not covered.
