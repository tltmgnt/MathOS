## 1. Result and scope

Use the nine-group normalized selector family, n=19m-7, L=Theta(m^3), arbitrary selected negative cross-clause bits F_H on supports H of width 1..3, and the fixed one-hot/OR/pin/guard extension. Choose g and three conditioning groups K. Define beta=sum_c |C_c|/m^4 and delta=sum_(c,a,H containing g) d_H,c(a,p_c(a))/m^6 for nonempty contextual sets C_c and maps p_c into them, where d_H,c is the parent's directed loss weighted by m^(2-|H minus ({g} union K)|). A wide support H containing g is a triple with two other groups outside K. Call it active if its raw rows indexed by g are not all identical (equivalently some directed loss is positive). For each active wide H put P=H union K (six groups).

The new structural hypothesis is a CERTIFIABLE residual cube cap. For every active wide H and every original ternary support B not contained in P, every aligned dyadic cube of side t in the padded domain of B contains at most t^(5/2) selected forbidden records. This cap applies to all scales t=1,2,4,...,M where M is the least power of two >=m; padding units have width one and do not affect the cap. No bound on the density of original ternary supports contained in P, no constraint on the geometry of the width-six exception itself, and no sparse projection hypothesis.

There is ONE fixed deterministic original three-symbol single-tape witness machine, correct throughout the nine-group family, which attains T(L)=o(L^3), visited span O(L polylog L), on ANY sequence of SAT inputs admitting a choice g,K satisfying the residual cube cap and competitor maps with beta_*->0, delta_*->0, arbitrarily slowly. It computes its own full-average score sigma=beta+delta on eligible choices, with charged bound for sufficiently small positive sigma

    O(m^(35/4) polylog(m+2) +
      m^9 sigma^(1/12) (1+log(1/sigma))^A)

for a fixed implementation-dependent integer A. No input-supplied choice, map, density or rate. This removes both global logarithmic obstructions of the parent on this structural subfamily, not on unrestricted selectors. General arbitrary-slow three-context loss recovery without the cap remains open.

More generally the same proof for any fixed rational p in [2,3) with cap t^p (or a fixed constant times it) gives a fixed machine with preparation O(m^((15+p)/2) polylog m), and density exponent (3-p)/6 in the same local-log expression. ## 6. Geometric local-log saving

Write R=|A_H| and rho=R/m^6. At any side t the number of positive full nodes is at most min((M/t)^9,R*(M/t)^3). Every visited descendant has a positive parent at 2t, so all visited nodes, including rejected children if counted separately, obey a constant multiple of this bound. Mandatory coarse root cost can also be charged separately by O((M/b)^9*b^(6+p) polylog b)=O(m^9/b^(3-p) polylog m)=O(m^((15+p)/2) polylog m). Empty guards have no positive descendant work. With R>=1 the descendant cost is at most a constant times the dyadic sum extended up to M:

    sum_t min(M^9*t^(p-3), R*M^3*t^(p+3))
          *(1+log(t+2))^A.

Split at t0=M/R^(1/6). On the small-scale side the second polynomial term decays geometrically as t decreases (p+3>0). On the large-scale side the first decays geometrically as t increases (p-3<0). The local logarithms at large scales contribute at most a fixed polynomial in the number of dyadic steps from the crossover. The resulting geometric series is summable, with constants depending on fixed p,A, not m or rho. Rounding t0 to a dyadic scale costs a constant factor. The bound is

    O(M^9*t0^(p-3)*(1+log(t0+2))^A)
       = O(m^9 rho^((3-p)/6)*(1+log(1/rho))^A).

This removes the critical global multiscale logarithm for this residual cap. It also gives unconditional O(m^9) traversal by summing the unpruned t^(p-3) local-log series. All density-independent preprocessing is strictly subcritical: the largest exponent is (15+p)/2<9, including the narrow solver's 42/5. Each branch density is <=sigma; monotonicity sufficiently near zero and the fixed number of branches give the main bound, with p=5/2 yielding 35/4 and 1/12. There is no global log m factor multiplying the density term. Span is O(m^3 polylog m) because b^6<=m^3, all original-scale caches fit that bound, and trees are processed one at a time. Both density-dependent preparation and critical local work have been avoided, not merely shifted onto a penalized score.

On the promise sigma->0, the resulting time is o(m^9)=o(L^3). The fixed actual description ell receives at least L^3/2^ell transitions at exact stage 3 ceil(log2 L), once eligible.
