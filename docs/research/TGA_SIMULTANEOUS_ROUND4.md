# Simultaneous research round 4 — scoped mathematical arguments

The following are mathematical derivations for restricted models, not claims of
novelty, external review, or solutions to the Millennium Problems. The code
executes exact arithmetic controls. Each step that transfers a result to a
full target remains an open obligation.

## RH: exact sharp signed-moment threshold
Let x_i be m distinct real nodes, z outside them, and L_i the Lagrange
basis. For polynomials p of degree at most m-1, p(z)=sum L_i(z)p(x_i).
Cauchy-Schwarz gives p(z)^2 <= (sum L_i(z)^2) sum p(x_i)^2, with equality
by choosing p(x_i)=L_i(z). Thus sum p(x_i)^2 - w p(z)^2 is nonnegative
for all such p exactly when w<=1/sum L_i(z)^2 (w>=0). For degree m,
p=product(t-x_i) yields a negative form for every w>0.
Transfer gap: this signed real measure is not the actual zeta-zero measure.

## P versus NP: exact separator counting
After processing variables 1 through i, retain those which appear in a clause
whose largest variable index exceeds i. A table indexed by assignments to
this frontier counts all processed partial assignments satisfying completed
clauses. Extend by both bits, check clauses ending at i+1, forget dead
variables and sum equal frontier states. The invariant follows by induction
because forgotten variables cannot appear in future clauses. Width w implies
at most 2^w states; transitions, clause evaluation and arithmetic on counts
of at most n+1 bits yield polynomial overhead times 2^w. This is not a
polynomial bound for arbitrary CNF; order selection and unbounded width remain.

## Navier–Stokes: energy-only estimate obstruction
On the normalized periodic torus, viscosity 1, the smooth velocity
u=(sin(k y) exp(-k² t),0,0) is divergence-free, has zero convection and solves
the heat equation with constant pressure. Its mean squared velocity at t=0
is 1/2; mean squared gradient Z(t)=k² exp(-2k²t)/2. Consequently the integral
of Z is 1/4, while the integral of Z² is k²/16 and the initial gradient
energy is k²/2. Fixed initial energy and energy dissipation do not give a
uniform bound on those stronger quantities. This is a family of globally
smooth solutions, not breakdown.

## Yang–Mills: exact finite-correlator nonidentifiability
For n>=2, put nodes x_j=(j+1)/(n+1), j=0,...,n. Define two probability
measures with weights binom(n,j)/2^(n-1), one on even j and one on odd j.
The nth finite difference annihilates polynomials of degree less than n,
so their moments at 0,...,n-1 match exactly; at degree n the difference is
(-1)^n n!/[2^(n-1)(n+1)^n]. One measure has positive mass at x=1 and
the other has maximum x=n/(n+1). With x=exp(-E), one support has gap zero
and the other strictly positive. Finite exact correlators alone do not
distinguish a gap in this abstract model. A physical gauge theory, vacuum
subtraction, dense observable family and uniform bounds are separate.

## Hodge: constructive primitive projector in a known algebraic ring
Use the square-zero algebra generated by the 2m factor classes of a product
of projective lines. L adds an unused generator and D deletes a generator.
On degree k, [D,L]=(2m-2k)I, by canceling off-diagonal terms. At middle
degree, D L^j D^j = L^j D^(j+1) + j(j+1)L^(j-1)D^j. Therefore the
coefficients a_j=(-1)^j/[j!(j+1)!] make D P=0 for
P=sum a_j L^j D^j, by telescoping. On ker D, P=I, so P is an idempotent.
L is adjoint to D in the orthonormal monomial basis; on middle degree the
commutator is zero, so ||Lv||²=||Dv||². Thus ker D=ker L there. This
constructs a projector onto primitive classes in this particular known
algebraic ring. No primitive algebraicity for general varieties follows.

## BSD: finite good-prime local ambiguity
For any finite set S of odd primes take M=8 product(S). There exist
positive nonsquare integers d congruent to 1 modulo M: take d=(M k)^2+M+1, which lies between
(M k)^2 and (M k+1)^2 for k>=1 and M>=2. It is 1 mod M.
Its squarefree part defines the same quadratic twist class and is a
quadratic residue at each p in S; none of these primes divides d.
The exact controls choose squarefree d directly by finite search for
specific sets. E_d:y²=x³-d²x and E_1 have isomorphic reductions at
those primes (the chosen controls even have identical coefficients).
This establishes finite local-data ambiguity, not distinct ranks or a
failure of BSD.

## Poincare: jump-product threshold in a scalar comparison
Let v>=0 decrease with slope at most -1 on unit intervals and jump by
a factor at most c_j>=1 at their endpoints. Divide by the product P_j
of previous jump bounds. The normalized quantity is nonincreasing at
jumps and loses at least 1/P_j per interval. If the sum diverges,
a nonnegative trajectory cannot persist indefinitely. For P_j=j+1
divergence follows from dyadic blocks each contributing at least 1/2.
For P_j=(j+1)^2 the sum converges by comparison with 1/[j(j-1)].
This is a conditional width-model criterion; actual surgery hypotheses
and comparison-map control must be proved for an independent reconstruction.
