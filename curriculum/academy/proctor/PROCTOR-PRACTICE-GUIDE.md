# TGA-PROCTOR-501 — practice examination and proctor answer guide

These are public worked training items, NOT a hidden secure exam bank. For summative assessment use equivalent independently authored variants; keep them private until the assessment. Score reasoning and explanation rather than answer memorization.

## Oral practice (10 points; 2 per item)
1. Is Poincaré one of the six unresolved targets? Explain. Answer: no, established externally; local reconstruction is distinct.
2. What does a modeled completion set establish? Answer: conditional sufficiency within the authored AND/OR graph; not mathematical necessity, proof or probability of solution.
3. Can positive 2×2 xi Hankel matrices establish RH? Answer: no, criterion quantifies all dimensions; finite positivity does not close it.
4. What does NO_OBVIOUS_FLAW establish? Answer: only that the AI critic reported no obvious flaw, not independent verification.
5. If a model suggests a Lean proof but no checker ran, what should be recorded? Answer: formalization plan only; NOT_PERFORMED for verification.

## Mathematical critique (10 points)
A candidate asserts: every individual p-primary Sha group is finite, so full Sha is finite. Identify the invalid inference (3), produce the abstract direct sum of Z/pZ countermodel and explain that this is not a claimed elliptic-curve Sha example (4), state the missing finite support or uniform annihilator condition (3).

## Dossier defense (10 points)
Give a pinned source and actual run manifest (3), explicit unproved hypothesis and correct classification (3), a revised next attack and falsifier (2), and disclose AI assistance and formal/review status honestly (2).
